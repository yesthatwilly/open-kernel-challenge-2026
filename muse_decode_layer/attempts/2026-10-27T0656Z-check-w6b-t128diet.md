## Deviations from the panel spec
- The norm / combine canary also accepts a cell that is at least as accurate as SGLang against fp64. SGLang's combine
  takes `post_expert_w` = bf16(1 + w) while ea_combine adds the 1 in fp32, so on real weights the two can differ by
  ~1.8e-3 rel (bf16 spacing near 1). The same `ea_combine` already runs judged at T=128 in Engine A's path.
- khalf / NN are checked against an fp64 reference (stricter than an fp32 split-K one), with a bound tied to cuBLAS's
  own error.
- Each cell's isolated ratio has to clear its bar in both rounds. NN 1.02 applies to the pooler at T=256 and the
  unpooler at T=128.
- The gate inputs are sliding verify batches. The cells do not depend on the attention, so no v16f-shaped input is
  timed separately.

## Risks / what could fail
- hilo's fp32 atomics are order-nondeterministic, so a near-tie expert pick can differ between replays. The copy check
  compares ids exactly: a flip would turn the T off (safe, but it could kill hilo by chance). The judge checks routing
  by its near-tie rules.
- ea_combine launched with PDL after cold K2: its CTAs may become resident early and wait at griddepcontrol.wait.
  That is the same arrangement as Engine A's judged cmb_t path.
- Memory: about +136 MB per state for the NN / hi-lo copies, and about twice that during the copy check.
- prepare grows by roughly 10-25 s: fp64 reference GEMMs, about 16 graphs per T, 3 layer-graph pairs per T and the
  copy check. The first hilo / add-cast calls compile Triton (a few seconds), and the _DL TU's compile grows by one
  small kernel.
- In-decode GEMMs have run slower than standalone before (secondary_levers O5). The isolated bars and the whole-layer
  gate decide.
- Size headroom is 10 bytes. Stacking with W6-A / W6-C will cross 990,000, and build.py will then strip comments (the
  AST is unchanged).
- A `layer check T=... FAIL` line carries `_cb_cmp_layer`'s full message (~270 chars), so FINAL PATHS cuts it at 200
  (the hidden / stream / update errors survive; the routing-rule text may not). PATH CHOSEN still says `layer check FAIL`.
- A device fault inside a cell would fail the run. The canary launches every cell eagerly and synced first, so the
  fault would be named. No board effect: this is a check.
