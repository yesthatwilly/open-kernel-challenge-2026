# dl-p-wide-tile-t16-t32

## Change

Kernel P (dl_pooler_router_kernel<NF>) streams only 2.85 TB/s at T=32 and 3.41 at T=8 (prepare timing in 81ee6714b7614a99.log: 26.4 us vs 20.7 at T=1) because each 64-row CTA re-reads its x K-chunk from L2: x is NF x 2 KB per 16 KB weight stage (50% extra at NF=4). For NF = 2 and 4 only (T 9..32) instantiate a wide variant: BLOCK_N 128 weight rows per CTA (8 warps x 16 rows, THREADS 256), BLOCK_K 64 per stage (W stage 16 KB, x stage NF x 1 KB), 4 stages = 72/80 KB so 2 CTAs/SM; grid 36 n-tiles x SPLIT 6 (K-chunks of 21/21/21/21/21/23 x 64) = 216 CTAs in one wave; x bytes per weight byte halve. Router tiles stay 2 x [16 hi; 16 lo] pairs inside a 128-row tile (tile layout of W_pr unchanged: rows 4096..4607 = 4 wide tiles of 2 router sub-tiles each), split-K ticket per wide tile, same fixed-order reduce and epilogue mapping, ws sized SPLIT 6. PDL pre-wait prefetch of stages 0-2 unchanged. NF=1 (T <= 8) keeps the current kernel. Self-check: the existing P checks at T 9, 16, 32 (ei rel L2 <= 2e-3, logits vs fp64); print occupancy and P timing for the wide variant at T=16/32; fall back per NF to the current kernel if occupancy < 2 or the check fails.

## Expected

- `*load=decode_32 *` faster: P 26.4 -> ~21.5 us (3.5 TB/s): ~5 us of 247/535 (+2% sliding)
- `*load=decode_16 *` faster: P 23.9 -> ~21 us: ~3 us of 217 (+1.3%)
- `*load=decode_1 *` flat: NF=1 unchanged: control
- `*load=verify_*` flat: T=8 NF=1 and T>32 unchanged: control

## Risk

Medium: register pressure at 8 warps x NF=4 fragments; if occupancy drops to 1 CTA/SM the 216-CTA grid takes two waves and loses. Small headline effect (~+0.5%), judge on the P timing line and the decode_16/32 rows.

**Verdict: proceed.** Plausible, self-gated, shape-scoped change to kernel P at NF=2/4 only (T 9..32). The NF=1 shapes and T>32 shapes act as in-run controls. ### Justification
The data support the target. The prepare timing (job 81ee6714) gives P at 20.7 us at T=1, 21.9 at T=8 and 26.4 at T=32 (2.85 TB/s), and the profiles show P<2> at 25.1 us (sliding decode_16) and P<4> at 27.0/26.6 us (decode_32), against ~15.7 us for 75.5 MB at the 4.8 TB/s floor. So 4-5 us is recoverable, and REWRITE_READ lists this exact change as stage item 3. The geometry checks out on master's source: K=8192 is 128 blocks of 64, split 21x5+23=128; 4608 rows give 36 wide tiles, so 216 CTAs as today; smem is 4x(16 KB + NF KB) = 72/80 KB, 2 CTAs/SM fits; launch_bounds(256,2) allows 128 regs, and the NF=4 accumulator is only ~16 regs/lane. The expected directions fit the weights: decode_32 sliding -5/247 is about 2%, full decode_32 and full decode_16 are under 1% and will read as flat, and decode_1/verify are controls. WEAKEST LINK: the diagnosis. 'x re-read from L2' is asserted, not shown. x adds ~37.7 MB of L2 traffic over 26 us (~1.4 TB/s), well below H200 L2 bandwidth. Master's NF=2/4 already runs at 2 CTAs/SM (occupancy [3,2,2]), and the 216-CTA grid places at most ~2 CTAs per SM at every NF. So the T-dependence more likely comes from per-stage consumer time: x ldmatrix plus NF times the mma per W fragment, with only 8 warps per SM to hide it. The wide variant also changes that, since it doubles the warps per SM (8 -> 16) and halves the K per stage. A gain is therefore a real gain of the variant, but it does not prove the L2 re-read story. The residual risk is a wash: 4 stages of 64-K hold half the K-depth in flight per CTA of today's 4 x 128-K. Bytes in flight per CTA stay the same (16 KB/stage), but a 128-row CTA now needs the same ring to cover twice the rows.

### Confounders on the current kernel
- small-t-launch-minimal-dense-chain-pdl: P is this hypothesis's own kernel, so this is the base, not a confounder. Its PDL pre-wait prefetch of stages 0-2 is kept. On master the predecessor is SGLang's attn_boundary, which never triggers launch_dependents (REWRITE_READ: PDL bought 0.3 us). The P row is therefore not inflated by griddepcontrol.wait time, and the measurement on master is clean.
- stack-align-par-sidestream-sliding1: it touches T>32 dispatch and sliding batch-1 attention only. It does not touch P at T 9..32, so it is not a confounder.
- The other landed items (marlin-block-m-cap-16, fa3 pairing, router-hilo parity, post-expert-norm) do not touch kernel P's launch or traffic. One indirect path: P's split-K sum order changes (SPLIT 6 vs 3), so logits differ in the last bits and could flip near-tie routing into Marlin. That is tolerance-only, and Marlin's +-20-40 us profile noise dwarfs any timing effect, so the Marlin rows must not be read as part of this result.

### Interference and A/B needs
No companion is needed. A gain shows on the P<2>/P<4> rows of decode_16/32. A loss is explained by the logged occupancy and fallback lines (occupancy < 2 sends the change back to the current kernel per NF). The only within-change ambiguity is mechanism attribution (x re-read vs warps per SM vs K-depth per stage). That does not affect the land/no-land decision, so a control is not worth a check. INTERACTION WITH THE RUNNING CHECKS (dl-route-warp-topk-pdl b471a950, boundaries-unpooler-pdl-small-t 1dfe83fd, and dense-chain-stage2-attn-side-pdl b31b8453), which all edit the same _DL_CUDA TU: (1) dl-route-warp-topk-pdl inserts griddepcontrol.launch_dependents into dl_pooler_router_kernel after the K loop and PDL-launches R. If it lands first, the wide variant must get the same griddep_launch() after its K loop, or the R overlap is lost at T 9..32 and the restack reads as a loss on R, not P. (2) boundaries-unpooler-pdl-small-t makes P's predecessor fire launch_dependents first. P then starts early, and its profiled duration includes the wait. P rows measured after that lands are not comparable with rows measured before it. That is the main reason to run this now on the current master rather than wait. The wide variant's pre-wait prefetch is 3 x 16 KB per CTA x 216 CTAs, the same 10.4 MB as today, so it composes with the boundary PDL. (3) dense-chain-stage2 renames the module (v2) and adds Q/O kernels cloned from P. Whichever lands later needs a textual 3-way restack, not a re-measure. Do not wait on any of them. The three are mutually conflicting textually but independent in what they measure.

## Implementation notes

Edited only `.worktrees/muse_decode_layer/dl-p-wide-tile-t16-t32/muse_decode_layer/submission.py` (+367/-12). No GPU/nvcc on the build node: nothing was compiled or run.

- New kernel `dl_pooler_router_wide_kernel<NF>` (NF = 2, 4 only) in the `_DL_CUDA` TU, a distinct symbol (review open question 1). 128 weight rows per CTA (8 warps x 16), BLOCK_K 64, 4 stages = 72 / 80 KB, `__launch_bounds__(256, 2)`, grid 36 n-tiles x 6 K-chunks = 216 CTAs. Same instruction forms as master's P (cp.async.cg, ldmatrix.x4, mma.sync m16n8k16), same XOR swizzle (8 chunks per 128 B row), same PDL pre-wait weight prefetch of stages 0-2, same split-K ticket and fixed-order reduce (p = 0..5). Router tiles: rows 4096.. are 4 wide tiles, expert = 64 x (tile - 32) + sub x 16 + j; W_pr layout unchanged.
- Deviation from spec: the K-chunk split uses the master formula `kb = s * 128 / 6` = 21/21/22/21/21/22 blocks, not 21 x 5 + 23. Same sum, better balanced.
- Master's P is untouched and still serves T <= 8. `dl_pooler_router` takes a new `wide` bit mask (2 = NF 2, 4 = NF 4); `_dl_forward` zeroes it for T > `DL_WIDE_MAX_TOKENS` (new constant, = 32, separate from `DL_MAX_TOKENS`, which is not edited). `ws` is allocated for SPLIT 6 (`DL_WIDE_SPLIT_K`); `cnt_p` already has 72 counters.
- prepare(): `_dl_wide_info` prints regs, local/spill bytes (cudaFuncGetAttributes), smem, CTAs/SM and warps/SM (at the occupancy limit and as resident for the real grid on this GPU's SM count) for old and wide P at NF=2/4, so the 8 -> 16 warps/SM lever is visible next to the 2 CTAs/SM number. `_dl_wide_check` runs old vs wide P at T 9/12/16 and 17/24/32, two launches each, with a flushed print before and after a `torch.cuda.synchronize()` for every launch; passes at ei rel L2 <= 2e-3 and logits max-rel <= 1e-4 vs master's P (bit-exactness is printed, not required, since the split-K order differs) and counters all reset. Then the existing full `_dl_self_check` (fp64 logits, R, Marlin y, graph replay at T=32) is rerun with the wide path on. Per NF fallback to master's P if occupancy < 2, the check fails or raises, or the full self-check fails. `_dl_time_wide` prints CUDA-event graph timings (20 launches x 5 replays, pdl=0) of old vs wide P at T=16 and 32. Prepare timings undercount the profile by 20-25% (LESSONS 2026-10-25): rank on them, judge on the profile row.
- `PATH CHOSEN for P:` line per shape class (T<=8, 9..16, 17..32). `-lineinfo` added to the nvcc flags. The wide self-check never raises into prepare().
- Review fixes applied: distinct symbol; old and wide timed in the same run at T=16/32 (T=9 is covered by the check, not the timing); fallback per NF on occupancy < 2. Not applied (not in this change): `griddepcontrol.launch_dependents` after the K loop, which the review says the wide kernel must get only if dl-route-warp-topk-pdl lands first.

## Pre-flight audit

Audited 2026-10-25 by an opus subagent, by reading the code. **Verdict: dispatch.** No fixes were applied.

- K split: kb = s*128/6 gives 0/21/42/64/85/106/128, so the 21/21/22/21/21/22 blocks cover all 128 K-blocks exactly once, and every chunk is >= 3 stages. The swizzle, the A/B ldmatrix.x4 and the mma m16n8k16 fragments match master P with 8 chunks per row (conflict-free). issue_w/issue_x index counts are correct for NF 2/4. The ws write and reduce strides agree (6 x MAXT x ROWS, allocated as max(3,6)). The router remap gives expert 64*(ntile-32)+sub*16+j, which equals master's 32*mtile+sub*16+j layout.
- Split-K ticket: the same fence/sync/atomicAdd/fence/ldcg pattern as master, a fixed p=0..5 reduce order, and an in-kernel atomicExch reset, so it is graph-replay safe. It has no spin, so it cannot hang.
- Build: no conditional over template-ids. kernel_info<F> resolves to the runtime template overloads, and no new symbols collide. There is no ptxVersion gate.
- Smem: 72/80 KB per CTA, so 2 CTAs/SM fits. launch_bounds(256,2) caps registers at 128, and acc is at most 32 floats.
- Gates: occupancy >= 2, the wide-vs-old check (ei 2e-3, logits 1e-4) and the full self-check (T 9/16/32, graph replay at T=32) are all passable. Any miss falls back to master P, and the PATH CHOSEN line prints.
- Rules: kernel_fn branches on T only, does no host sync, caches nothing from data, and leaves DL_MAX_TOKENS untouched. Minor scope creep: -lineinfo was added to the nvcc flags (harmless).

Residual risk: never compiled. A device fault in the wide check poisons the context for the whole run, which no in-process fallback can catch. A full-self-check miss turns off both NFs together, not per NF.
