# c320-combine-onepass

## Change

Stacked on merge-perkvhead-v3chain-sv32-tiled (its branch carries the c320 module muse_full_decode_c320f, GATE A and the V0..V4 chain gate; claim restacks its patch onto master). Full decode_1 there is 17.77 us of shape for c320_attn_kernel<320, true> 11.4 us + c320_combine_kernel<320, 16, PDL> 4.8 us: the combine adds ~5 us after kernel A. ONE change, the body of c320_combine_kernel (template <CHUNK, NW, PDL>, grid (B, HQ), NW = 16 warps, and the PDL pre-wait reads of seqlens / v_scale / gate stay as they are). Today: pass 1 takes the max of the n partials' m with a block reduce (one L2 round trip + __syncthreads), pass 2 has warp w fold c = w, w + NW, ... with #pragma unroll 4 (about 4 dependent L2 round trips per warp at n = 251). New, ONE pass: each warp issues the loads of a group of up to 16 of its partials (c = w + NW * i, i < 16, guarded by c < n; ws_ml m and l of head h, float2 of ws_acc at lane * 2) fully unrolled, every load before any use, then folds them in registers with an online LSE (running M, rescale L and acc by ex2(M_old - M_new); an m = -inf partial weighs 0; -inf safe as now); the group loop repeats only when n > NW * 16 = 256 (contexts above ~82K keys), so any length stays correct. Then each warp writes (M_w, L_w, acc_w) to shared memory, one __syncthreads, and warp 0 merges the NW warp results with the same LSE weights and runs the unchanged epilogue (o / L * v_scale, scaleless RMSNorm over 64 with eps inside the sqrt, sigmoid(gate), bf16 out). Same arithmetic up to fp32 summation order. V1..V4 all use this combine, so GATE A (stored-row and output checks, two-state replays at 80000 and 4097 keys) and the V0..V4 timing gate (route the best only if best / V0 <= 0.98) re-run unchanged and pick the route; read the 'PATH CHOSEN: full decode_1 [chain]' line (V1..V4 vs V0) and c320_combine_kernel's profile time.

## Expected

- `*layer=full load=decode_1 *` faster: combine 4.8 us (PDL wait included) on a 17.77 us shape: ~5 dependent L2 round trips -> ~2; expect -1.5..-2.5 us at 0.34%/us (+0.5-0.85%)
- `*layer=full load=decode_16 *` flat: c320 routes batch 1 only
- `*layer=sliding *` flat: untouched

## Risk

Below the 1.24% one-run margin alone: read the FD1 row and the chain gate's V3/V4 times against v3chain's (V3 19.39, V4 19.36 us in b4b20425); it stacks with sk-full-line-gather (disjoint rows) and c320-pdl-early-trigger (same row, separate mechanism). Risk low: GATE A rejects a wrong combine and the chain falls back to V0 (FD1 ~22.4 us: a bug, not a result). Decode-portable: the c320 chain is the planned d1f port into decode's piece A.
