# wg2-w13-occ2-nmax128-stg2-hot

## Change

w13 tile shape only, at B256 on the restacked wg2 path (restack-occ2-wg2-b256-two-stream-halves-on-master); w2 keeps wg2_kernel<false, 64, 3, 2, ORD> unchanged, and every other cell stays the base. MECHANISM: with NMAX 64 the judge's hot experts (~130 local routes at T=256 under _wg_judge_ids) are cut into 3 N-chunks, and each chunk re-streams and RE-DEQUANTIZES the same 128-row weight tile over the full K=4096, so the hottest experts' w13 bytes are read from DRAM/L2 and dequantized 2-3 times (the occ2 reviewer's unminted follow-up; DIAGNOSIS action 5). CHANGE: add one instantiation wg2_kernel<true, 128, 2, 2, ORD> (NMAX 128, 2 stages, 2 CTAs/SM; ~110 KB smem per CTA so two fit in 228 KB) used for w13 only, with _wg_chunks cut at 128 rows. Registers: N128 needs 64 fp32 accumulators per consumer thread, which does not fit the base's setmaxnreg 104 with a0[8][4] (32 regs) live; so (a) move to setmaxnreg producer 24 / consumers 112 (128x24 + 256x112 = 31744 regs per CTA, x2 = 63488 <= 65536), and (b) in this instantiation dequantize one 64-k record at a time (a[4][4], 16 regs) with two commit/wait<0> groups per stage instead of one. ptxas must report 0 spill bytes for the new instantiation (print the -Xptxas -v line); if it spills, the prepare gate keeps the base w13. Gate: cold-L2 graph A/B of the full _moe_wgmma path, w13 N128 vs base N64, on _wg_judge_ids and zipf a=0.9 routings, require >= 1.02x on the worse; print chunks per kernel (expected 3 -> 2 for the hottest experts) and w13 kernel-only TB/s. Canary per N bucket (16/32/64/128) with finiteness and judge-bound relative L2. First measurement is an okc check.

## Expected

- `bs=256 *` faster: w13 wg2 row is 225.3 us at verify_32; removing the 2-3x re-read/re-dequant of the hottest experts is -5 to -10% on it = -11 to -22 us, +0.5-1.0% headline. Below the 1.24% margin alone: lands only stacked with wg2-pingpong-consumers-occ2-b256 or the restack itself
- `bs=128 *` flat: B128 stays Marlin (max ~44 local routes per expert, NMAX 64 already holds them)

## Risk

Medium risk: register pressure (spills would erase the gain) and two wait<0> per stage add serialization that may cost what the de-duplication saves; the printed chunk counts and TB/s attribute it. Prefill LESSONS 175: TLP needs the full 128-route slab to pay under skew, which is this change.
