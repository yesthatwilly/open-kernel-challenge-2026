## Arms (all on A5b codes + packed scale + D)

| arm | change vs A5b+D+SC |
|---|---|
| **RS2** | Rolling split commit: the stage's two groups (record 0 / record 1) are never fully drained inside an item. Before group g's dequant, `wgmma_wait<1>` retires exactly the previous stage's group g, so stage s+1's record-0 dequant runs under stage s's record-1 wgmma; the previous stage is released after its last group retires. No extra registers, no accumulator fence in the window. SASS: `WARPGROUP.DEPBAR.LE gsb0, 0x1` in the loop, clean. This is idea (a) without needing A5b's freed registers. |
| **RS4** | Same with 4 groups of 2 k16 (`wait<3>`, half-record dequant from uint2 code loads). |
| **RS2+MC** | RS2 + 2-CTA cluster: items 2P / 2P+1 share chunk and expert (j = 2jj, 2jj+1); each CTA TMA-loads half of the B slab with `.multicast::cluster` mask 3 (tx unchanged), empty barriers count 16 with a `mapa` remote arrive (CUTLASS form; the `.release.cluster` form cost a MEMBAR per stage: x1.6-2.1), cluster barrier at init and exit. 66 clusters fit (grid 132). Idea (b). |

## ncu of the variants

- **RS2** removes the barrier stall: high w13 cycles 968k -> 940k (-2.9%), tensor 78.7 -> 81.1%; ft tensor 85.3%. At low it
  is cycle-neutral (386.5k -> 385.9k): the stall moves to short/long scoreboard and wait. Low hbm loses 4.5%: with the
  previous stage held one group longer the producer has less slack on the HBM-like bank.
- **RS4**: +4% issued instructions (two LDS.64 per record + 2 more waits per stage), tensor 75%: loses everywhere.
- **RS2+MC** drops L2 throughput 80% -> 51% at ft, but cycles stay at 4.44M and tensor at 81%: **L2 is not the ft/high
  limiter**; the pair lockstep costs 4-14% at low/high.

## Verdict (per the rule)

**No bit-exact variant reaches >= 5% on the pair in any class -> no prefill check candidate.** Best: RS2 at high
(0.959-0.963, w2 0.955), under the bar; it loses at low hbm (1.045). A route-by-N rider (RS2 only for N >= 64, which
leaves low's N16/32 chunks on SC) would be ~4% on high only; at the A5b transfer that is <= ~1% headline: below the
value bar, shelf.

What bounds each class now:
- **low:** per-warp dependency latency with 2 consumer warps per SMSP (0.73 eligible warps per scheduler, 4.4
  cycles/issue, issue 52%, tensor 33%). Overlap levers are exhausted (RS2 neutral, RR refuted in rgx2); the
  remaining lever would be more consumer TLP (a third consumer WG = an M=192 tile and bank re-tiling) or fewer
  dequant instructions (the encoding floor was 9 ops/word, rgx2).
- **high / ft:** the tensor pipe at 79-85% active with RS-wgmma (A from registers); L2 (72-80%) is not binding (MC proved
  it), and the wgmma-wait bubble is mostly gone with RS2. The headroom left is ~15-20% to a 100%-active tensor pipe,
  which these schedule levers do not reach.
- The next prefill lever is outside the rega tile's dequant/schedule (rega-1029 / anat: turn_1 sliding's other rows), or
  a tile reshape for low TLP.
