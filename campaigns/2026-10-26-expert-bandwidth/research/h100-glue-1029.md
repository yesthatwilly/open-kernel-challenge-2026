**Question (pre-registered):** how much of the expert block is non-GEMM glue (router/top-k, align, gather, combine, PDL gaps)
per T class, and is there a legal cut worth >= 5% of the expert block in some class with >= 1% moe headline?
**Decision rule:** yes -> moe check candidate; otherwise this anatomy goes to the 10-30 plan.

## Verdict: NO moe check candidate. The glue is too small to clear the bar in any class.

- Only T=1 and T=256 have >= 5% glue, and neither can be cut to zero. The best measured legal cut is x1.016 at T=256
  (`cp`: ea_combine replaces the hm post-norm). That is ~0.25% headline on the node and ~0.15-0.2% expected on the judge.
- Ceiling: zero-cost align + gather plus `cp` at T=256 gives about x1.06 for the row, which is <= x1.010 headline
  (row elasticity 1/6, per hyp.py triage). Reaching that would need a free align, which is not possible.
- Router / top-k is not in moe's timed call (topk_ids arrive as input). Graph launch gaps are ~0 in every class:
  0.6-0.85 us at T=256 and 0.00 elsewhere.

## Side finding (important): master moe 09b11a9d crashes in prepare on gpunode06

The run failed 2 of 2 times (20261029-160154 under nsys, 20261029-160455 plain). Both runs logged:
`[ea-enc] raised AcceleratorError: CUDA error: an illegal memory access was encountered -> master everywhere`.
The error is sticky, so the context is poisoned and prepare() fails (`torch.cuda.empty_cache` raises). It comes from
`_eae_run`'s per-T E-encoding race (`_eae_class`), after the recode line.

The node's routes differ from the judge's: T=1 = v1 here, v6pe2 on the judge. The first E class raced on the node is
T=8 (v4xs). The judge passed twice (24f183ff, 702f1004).

If the cause is route-dependent, a final rerun whose ea6 race picks v1 at T=1 could fail the whole moe run. Triage
this before the final submission window. Reproduce with `HP_BUNDLE=njglue bin/hopper-probe run gpunode06 --gpus 4
nodejudge-gl-plain`. All results above use `CAMPAIGN_EAE = False`, which slows only the cold half's encodings
(~2.5% of the cold-half time) and leaves the glue unchanged.

## For the 10-30 plan
- Glue is not a lever: <= 3% at T=8..128, ~8% at T=1 (fixed-cost combine, EPF shelved) and ~7-8% at T=256.
- The T=256 serial head/tail (align + post-norm, ~16-21 us) is the only sizeable glue. To cut it: (a) `cp` (proven on
  the node, rider); (b) a multi-CTA CUDA align with a PDL trigger into gather/ea4x, worth <= 2.5% of the row, which
  serves decode's `_hc_align` too. Together they are <= ~0.6% moe headline, so they only pay as riders on a hot-half stack.
