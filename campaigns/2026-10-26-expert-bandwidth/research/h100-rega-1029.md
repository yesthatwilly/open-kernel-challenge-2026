## Question (pre-registered by the main thread)

Why is prefill's rega expert tile "super-serial" (judge cb22e610, low w13 warm pop: t0 293.6, MMA-only t1 194.4,
dequant-only t2 191.1, producer-only t4 145.1 us)? Working read: per-SMSP contention between dequant ALU work and RS
wgmma (register-file read ports / issue), not latency. Decision rule: full-mode tensor-active << MMA-only with
math-pipe-throttle / dispatch stalls dominant -> contention confirmed, lever = fewer dequant instructions (dq2) or dequant
off the consumer SMSPs; A3 cuts full mode >= 8% on the node -> port to the rega tile and the moe hot half (10-30
phase A). Long-scoreboard / barrier dominant instead -> ring / producer is the bound.

## Method

- **Source = master's, generated, not retyped.** `gen.py` AST-reads master submission.py (blob c63801e7; asserts
  REGA_V2_SHA256), applies REGA_FSILU + REGA_W2ST_EDITS (the fsilu_w2a source master dispatches), the anatomy r2
  REGA_ANAT_EDITS (MODE 0-5, cb22e610's module) and rgx edits: **MODE 6 = pure dequant issue** (no producer, no ring
  waits, dq_stage on stale smem kept live by mode 2's predicated-store sink, no epilogue; it is the dq2 hypothesis'
  "MODE 5") and `dq_stage_x<RGX_DQ>`: 0 = master's dq_stage verbatim, A1 = paired scale (one-source prmt [b_g,0,b_g8,0]
  + mad.lo t*m16+0x77807780, m16 = rt16(KS); multiply via moe's mul_bf16x2_h .H0/.H1), A2 = moe ext_prop on a
  host-permuted copy of the code words (moe _ea_luts index 2), A3 = A1 + A2. All four arms in one binary.
- **Shapes and routing as the judge.** K 4096, KS 32, n_tiles 64 (w13) / 32 (w2), grid 132, 32 local experts, master's
  _rega_pop_case (Gumbel top-8 of 256, alpha 1 at low T 753 -> 726-758 local routes, alpha 2 at high T 3012 -> 5189-5211;
  judge 728 / 5169), expert-sorted blocks of 16, chunks of 8 blocks. Low chunk mix N16/32/64/128 = 14-16/12/2-4/2.
- **Two banks.** `hbm` = 32 distinct experts (judge bytes; H100 HBM). `l2` = every expert aliased onto ONE 18.9 MB
  physical unit with the CUDA VMM API, so the ring is fed from L2: this removes HBM and isolates per-SM behaviour.
- **Exactness.** Every arm's mode-0 output vs master, bitwise, 5 configurations x 2 GEMMs x 2 runs: **all eq, 0 diff**,
  error word 0, launch markers ok. 168 regs, 0 local, occ 1 in every arm (same as master).
- **Timing.** 7 interleaved shuffled rounds x 10 back-to-back launches per (arm, mode, gemm); medians, round spread
  < 1%. No CUDA graph (launch gaps of a few us are in every arm alike). Clocks not locked (1785-1830 MHz under load).
- **ncu** 2026.2.1 (nvhpc 26.9), `--clock-control none`, WarpStateStats / SchedulerStats / InstructionStats /
  ComputeWorkloadAnalysis / SourceCounters + pipe metrics, one launch per target after 2 warm launches, 21 targets.
- **SASS** cuobjdump -sass of the cu130 build (cuda-13.0.1, -O3 -std=c++17 -lineinfo, sm_90a); `rgx_sass.py` finds each
  inner k loop.

## Result 1: the timing anatomy (H100, medians, us)

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| low l2 w13 | 290.5 | 155.5 | 204.3 | 115.9 | 158.2 | 0.93 |
| low l2 w2 | 150.2 | 85.6 | 103.0 | 61.1 | 86.3 | 0.87 |
| low hbm w13 | 293.6 | 230.9 | 234.9 | 205.0 | 158.7 | (t1 ring-bound) |

| high l2 w2 | 373.4 | 306.6 | 225.9 | 231.8 | 152.1 | 0.81 |
| judge H200 low w13 (cb22e610) | 293.6 | 194.4 | 191.1 | 145.1 | - | - |

- `[cyc]` **t0 is SM-bound at low:** H100 t0 = 290.5 (L2 bank) / 293.6 (HBM bank) vs the judge's 293.6, within 1%,
  while t1 / t2 / t4 move with the memory system (the judge's sit between our two banks). So t0's anatomy transfers.
- `[cyc]` **It is serial, not super-serial.** With the ring removed from the dequant arm (t6), t0 = 0.93 x (t6 + t1) at
  low w13, 0.81-0.87 elsewhere: dequant and MMA run one after the other with 7-19% overlap. The judge's "t0 / (t1 + t2
  - t5) = 1.29" read the ring twice: t1 and t2 both carry ring waits (t2 = 204 here vs pure dequant 158), and
  subtracting t5 once does not remove them.

  are identical (508.4k / SM low, 3.05M high), so the wgmma itself is not stretched: the pipe sits idle while dequant runs.
- Full-mode stall samples (all warps, low): selected 29.2%, wait 22.9%, not_selected 12.4%, barrier 11.8% (all on
  WARPGROUP.DEPBAR = the per-stage wgmma wait<0>), long_scoreboard 8.2% (the producer's mbarrier spin at BRA),
  math_pipe_throttle 5.1%, short_scoreboard 4.1%, dispatch 3.2%. **Math-throttle + dispatch = 8%: not dominant.**
- Which stalls grow in full vs MMA-only + pure dequant (warp-cycles per SMSP, low): gmma (= WARPGROUP.ARRIVE wait, the
  4 warps of a WG waiting for the slowest one's dequant) +49k, long_scoreboard +39k (producer spin, off the critical
  path), dispatch +25k. These shrink: barrier -36k, short_scoreboard -35k, math_pipe_throttle -21k, wait -9k. Total
  cycles -39k (the 7% overlap).
- `[cyc]` **High class is different: dispatch stalls are 297k warp-cycles / SMSP in full mode vs ~25k in MMA-only + no-MMA**
  (11.7% of samples; on the dequant IMAD 34%, LOP3 18%, HMUL2 17% of their own samples, HGMMA 20%). At N128, the other
  WG's in-flight RS wgmma reading its A registers holds back this WG's dequant dispatch: the RF-port contention the
  working read proposed is real at high / ft, but it is ~3% of time at low.
- Shared-memory bank conflicts are small (13.8k / launch). ncu on sm_90 exposes no register-bank-conflict counter;
  dispatch_stall is the nearest proxy.
- `[cyc]` A3's saving in full mode: 79.2k cycles / SM for 63.5k fewer issued warp-instructions / SMSP (1.25 cycles per
  removed instruction). In pure dequant it saves 53.7k for the same 63.5k (0.85). The dequant sits on the serialized
  critical path, and its cuts show up more than 1:1 in t0 (stall deltas: selected -63k, not_selected -31k, wait -23k,

## Result 3: SASS, dequant instructions per k16 step (cu130, per consumer thread)

From the pure-dequant (mode 6) loop body / 8 k16 steps, sink and loop control excluded; the mode-0 loop is the same + 1
(27 / 23 / 23 / 19), plus 8 HGMMA and ~86 ring / wgmma / uniform-datapath instructions per stage.

| arm | per k16 | per stage (8 k16): main classes |
|---|---|---|
| master DQ0 | **26.0** | LOP3 64, IMAD.SHL 34, HMUL2 32, FMUL 16, HADD2.F32 16, F2FP 16 (8 E4M3 unpack + 8 BF16 pack), LEA.HI 8, SHF 14, LDS 4 |
| A1 (paired scale) | 22.0 | LOP3 64, IMAD.SHL 38, HMUL2 32, IMAD 10 (m16 mad), PRMT 8, LEA.HI 8, SHF 10, LDS 4 |
| A2 (ext_prop codes) | 22.0 | LOP3 48, HMUL2 32, IMAD.SHL 20, FMUL 16, HADD2.F32 16, F2FP 16, SHF 20, LDS 4 |
| A3 (dq2 = A1 + A2) | **18.0 (-31%)** | LOP3 48, HMUL2 32, IMAD.SHL 22, SHF 16, IMAD 10, PRMT 8, LDS 4 |

- The broadcast folds into HMUL2 `.H0_H0 / .H1_H1` in every arm (master's bf16x2_bcast too), and the `fma -0` becomes
  HMUL2.BF16_V2. A3's per-stage pipe mix per thread: ALU (LOP3 / SHF / PRMT) 72 vs master ~102; FMA (IMAD / HMUL2) 64
  vs ~100.
- RS operand pattern (master N32 loop): 8 back-to-back `HGMMA.64x32x16.F32.BF16 R24, R40|R44|...|R68, gdesc, R24`, each
  sourcing one 4-register quad of the 32 dequant outputs, accumulators chained in place, one commit group (`gsb0` on the
  8th), then `WARPGROUP.DEPBAR.LE gsb0, 0x0`. The 32 A registers must stay live until the group retires, so with one
  register set the next stage's dequant cannot start before that wait. This is the serialization.

## Verdict (per the pre-registered rule)

- **Tensor-active in full mode << MMA-only: yes** (25.5% vs 49.0% low, 66.3% vs 88.5% high).
- **Math-throttle / dispatch dominant: no at low** (8% of stall samples; issue, dependency wait and the per-stage
  wgmma wait dominate). **Yes in part at high**, where dispatch stalls grow by ~270k warp-cycles / SMSP over the
  parts (RS-wgmma RF contention).
- **Ring / producer bound: no.** t4 is 0.40 x t0 (L2 bank) and 0.70 x t0 even on H100's slower HBM. long_scoreboard is
  the producer warp's own spin. The barrier stalls are the wgmma wait<0>, not the ring.
- **Reading:** at low, the stage is issue-serialized: both consumer WGs dequant (issue-bound, 2 warps per SMSP), then
  the 8-wgmma chain and the wait. "Super-serial" was a modelling artifact (t1 + t2 - t5 counts the ring twice). The
  contention the working read proposed exists, but it is a high / ft effect. In both classes the lever is the same:
  **fewer dequant instructions on the consumer SMSPs.**
- **A3 cuts the full mode >= 8% on the node at low** (0.857 L2, 0.886 HBM; pair 0.857 / 0.888) -> per the rule,
  **port A3 (dq2 + L3/L4 paired scale) to the rega tile and the moe hot half (10-30 phase A)**. At high it is
  0.92 (w13) / 0.88-0.92 (w2): route it there too only if the judge diag confirms <= 0.97.
- Overlap schedules (K-pair, ping-pong, anti-phase) lost because the dequant phase is issue-bound on the same SMSPs:
  overlapping it with the other WG's MMA adds the dispatch contention seen at high, and nothing is left to hide. A
  dequant moved off the consumer SMSPs (the ssdq lineage) stays the only structural angle. Fewer instructions is the
  cheap one.

## Caveats

- Synthetic weights: uniform codes, every scale byte in [0x08, 0x7E] (A1 / A3's integer scale path needs that; the
  real bank must pass the prepare-time range check, as in moe). Timing is data-independent; exactness is proven on these
  data only (no exhaustive canary here; the dq2 hypothesis' review asks for one).
- The align is a host re-implementation (expert-sorted, sentinel = num_routes). It is not master's kernel; chunk
  histograms match the judge's form.
- No CUDA graphs; the H100 L2 bank's absolute t1 / t4 reflect H100 L2 bandwidth, not the judge's.
- Mode 6 runs dequant on stale smem (data-independent timing); its loop also carries the 8-store predicated sink (8 of
  ~223 instructions per stage).
