## What was ported

XL = X (ldmatrix.x4 B preload) + L (trimmed record loop), from eaenc2. **ea6's FAST consumer already runs X**: master's
`consume<., 1>` is the X1 preload. So only L is new at T=1. The port is ea6 template `int LT`, host flag bit 1024,
E4 / E5 codes:

- `consume_lt` (master's '4xl' text, unchanged) on code / scale shared addresses carried in registers. The B address
  is fixed for the whole CTA at T=1, and it and m16 / m78 are hoisted out of the loop.
- Ring slot / full / empty addresses step by one IMAD each (s = +1, or -(D - 1) at the wrap; D = 3 for K1, 2 for K2).
  The parity flips at the wrap, a count-down replaces i < r, and the full wait tries first.
- The P handoff (buffer i & 1) uses a carried `st.shared` address. The pfull / pfree addresses step by +-1. The pfree
  wait is unconditional with a carried parity: at i < 2, parity 1 of a fresh barrier is already complete.
- Record 0's wait, K1's griddep_launch and dbg[1] are peeled out of the loop.
- The records, order, arithmetic (cv = acc[0] + acc[1]) and handoff are the same as the E arm.

Registers (cu13.0, node env): K1 E4 70-71 -> E4XL 72, E5 64 -> E5XL 64, K2 the same. lmem 0. Occupancy is unchanged
(K1 1, K2 2), and K2 stays co-resident.

**Exactness:** at every n1..5, in all 4 runs, XL h / y / out == the E4 arm, bitwise (NaN-poisoned buffers). F5 and
F5XL == F as well, E == master encoding, KF == E, err 0.
