## 1. Methodology rules
**R1 Instrument.**
- Use gpunode06 only: queue GPUs 1, 3, 4, 5, 6, and GPU 7 for validations of 5 minutes or less.
- Jobs must say nodes=gpunode06. With nodes=h100 a job can land on an NVL node that has no env.
- Every record carries the MANIFEST sha256, the GPU, cold or warm caches, and the SM clock under load. A run below 1800 MHz, or one that throttles, is void.

**R2 Pre-registration.** Register each experiment in research/h100-findings.md before queuing it: the fields of §3, the expected effect and the bytes sha. Append results; never change a metric after the fact.

**R3 Estimators.**
- (a) Arms in one process: per rep, r = (base-ovh)/(arm-ovh) on identical inputs.
  - Report the median over judge pairs of sqrt(r[2i]·r[2i+1]).
  - CI: 95%, from 1000x bootstrap over pairs, stratified by routing features.
- (b) Different bytes or different prepares: isolated processes, at least 4 blocks.
  - Blocks alternate AB/BA, are balanced across GPUs, and use one seed per block.
  - Report the mean block log-ratio with a t-interval.
- One process's bootstrap never supports a cross-process claim.

**R4 Replicates.** At least 2 replicates, on different GPUs and seeds. Both must pass.

**R5 Nulls and controls.**
- Each instrument mode first passes a null and a positive control of known direction (M00, DEC-02, DEC-03).
- Each batch of 4 or more jobs carries a null. A null outside its band voids the batch.

**R6 MDE (minimum detectable effect).** These values are provisional until M00 and M1 report.
- Forced in-process arms: 0.3% per row.
- 6 isolated blocks: 0.6% per row and 0.25% on the headline.
- A result is resolved only if its CI clears 1 by at least the MDE. Otherwise it is "unresolved" and nothing changes.

**R7 Races.** Read every party's PATH CHOSEN lines before reading any row. A/Bs of code changes run with every other race pinned.

**R8 Transfer.**
- The model: t_J = 1.034·t_cons + 0.686·t_mem + t_lat.
  - The factors come from the SM clock (1830 -> 1769 MHz) and the HBM ring (3.05 -> 4.45 TB/s).
  - The row ratio ρ (node time / judge time) is 1.06-1.16 on the moe board.
- **Time-removing changes.** A change that only removes time keeps its sign. Its size scales by ρ(1.034 - 0.348x), which lies in [0.72, 1.20]. Here x is the memory share of the time removed.
- **Trade-off changes can flip sign.** These change the bytes read, a hot/cold threshold, the fork's SM partition, prefetch or L2 use, or overlap.
  - Such a change needs a judge-side reading, unless E3's time split predicts at least the MDE for x±0.2.
- Where x comes from:
  - per arm: X0b, x = (1.034 - 1/ρ_arm)/0.348;
  - per kernel: E3.

**R9 Judge checks.**
- One check resolves about 1.2% on the headline and about 3% per row, so the node decides.
- A check that carries pins only guards against regression:
  - the headline must stay at or above board - 1.2%;
  - no row may be more than 3% worse than predicted.
- Judge-side truth about an arm comes from prepare-time carriers.
  - A carrier replays many arms as paired graphs on the races' seeded routings, at least 41 reps each.
  - It prints compact lines at the end of the log tail.
- Pins on different rows go into one check. A scored run follows whenever a check beats the board.

**R10 Apply.**
- **Pin (E5)** only if all three hold:
  - resolved in both replicates;
  - same sign on at least 3/4 draws or strata;
  - safe under R8.
- **Tie** (difference below the MDE): pin the judge's majority arm from X0a. Where the log tail cannot show it, pin the node's majority arm. This ends the lottery and saves the race's prepare time.
- **Code change.** All of these, then one check:
  - an isolated A/B resolved;
  - a soak of at least 1000 checked replays per shape with 0 faults;
  - memcheck clean on its kernels;
  - the held-out sweep at 37/37.
- **Prepare-deadline change.** First run a cold node prepare of 240 s or less; the node compiles on 96 CPUs.

**R11 Correctness.** Every replay of every arm is checked against the reference. One miss voids the arm.

## 2. Enablers (one agent each, in its own files)
**E1 arm overrides + E1b routing control** (takemyjob probes/nodejudge/arms.py, mkexp.py, arms/{moe,decode}.json)
- Config: `arms: [{name, of, pre, post, cap}]`.
  - pre: module attributes set after import and before prepare(). The arm gets its own prepare and party.
  - post: copy-on-write edits to the base's prepared state. Tensors are shared; there is no new prepare.
  - cap: attributes set just before this arm's graph capture and restored afterwards (EA_PDL_BITS, _HMF_FK).
- Each arm is a party in the rotation and sees identical inputs.
  - It runs with copies=1, and every replay is checked.
  - One eager reference check runs before timing; an arm that fails it is excluded.
- Records:
  - the overrides and the state diff;
  - PATH lines and rep_features;
  - the R3a table against the base;
  - the SM clock;
  - the captured graph's kernel names (ctypes graph walk, or E3 as fallback).
- E1b: `routing {k_local: [...]}` produces exactly k distinct local experts at T=1 and T=8.
- mkexp.py turns a spec into configs (one seed per replicate), queue jobs and a ledger stub.
  - Jobs use nodes=gpunode06 and one GPU, and their prep step builds the bundle.
- arms/*.json lists every race and knob per T: path, legal values, when it is read (prepare, forward or capture), and prerequisites.

**E2 decode shim + E2b judge-like inputs** (shims.py, configs/decode-*.json)
- Shim these modules, written from reference.py:
  - FusedPostNormResidualPreNorm, FusedRMSNormGate, FusedMuseSparkRouting, FusedQKNorm (with RoPE);
  - the three muse_spark_layers classes.
- Shim requirements:
  - use the exact API that baseline.py and our prepare() call (kwargs, attributes, precompute_*, the 3-tuple boundary);
  - be graph-capturable;
  - use SGLang's kernel names.
- Also:
  - check that _bf16_gemm_dispatch_impl exists in sglang 0.5.22;
  - record the FA3 build;
  - log prepare's peak memory. The node has 80 GB against the judge's 141, and an out-of-memory fallback voids the run.
- Configs: per-shape repeats taken from the case (60 for decode 1, 20 otherwise) and the 13 tests. Variants: board (cold), iso, null, null-iso, sweep and margin.
- E2b: judge-like router draws through weights_fields' search.
  - The targets come from the judge logs' [combo] stream-basis lines and the SoL-implied router_distinct.
  - Accept a draw when its busiest-range routes and distinct experts per T are within 10% of the judge's.
- Validation: V1 = DEC-01; V2 and V3 = DEC-02; the null = DEC-03. If V3 fails, only arms that leave every shimmed op unchanged can be resolved on the node.

**E3 profiling** (profile.py)
- Capture: nsys --trace=cuda,nvtx --cuda-graph-trace=node over a 20-rep pass.
  - NVTX ranges mark party, shape and rep.
  - The trace is reduced on the node to a kernel table.
- The parser reports durations, gaps, stream overlap and the critical path.
  - For PDL chains, use critical-path differences, not kernel durations.
- The profiled row must be within 3% of the unprofiled row.
- ncu only if P1 passes, and always with --clock-control none.
- Never pause DCGM. Pausing it changes node monitoring and needs the user's explicit approval (add it to PENDING_USER).

**E4 analysis** (tools/)
- A race-line parser for node outputs and judge log tails, which builds the race ledger.
- The transfer table, an err/bound extractor and A/B summaries.
- E4 also runs X0a-c.

**E5 pins + carriers** (musespark campaign branch)
- RACE_PIN {race: arm} for both problems; decode already has HC_PIN and HCG_PIN.
  - A pinned race keeps its canary, layer and copy checks.
  - It falls back to racing on any miss and prints PINNED.
- The J0 and J1 carriers time the listed arms after every routing decision, within 60 s, and print a compact final block.

**Cut:**
- Exact decode input replay: the router targets are not visible.
- Pausing DCGM.
- M2: M00 and M1 answer it.
- Standalone R1: it falls out of the cold runs.
- DEC-07: the carriers measure it.
- M01, M02 and M03's partition knobs: they tune the wgmma hot half that S1a replaces; see C2.

**M07 — Does K1's w2 prefetch cut decode 1?** [5 · E1 · carrier]

**M456 — Which engine, cw, K2 grid and combine win per Engine A class?** [16 · E1 · carrier for arms above 1%]

  - T=8: v1, v4, v4c cw7/9, v4 with K2 132, v4 with the Triton combine.
  - T=16: v4, v4c cw7/8/9/11, v3, K2 132.

  - T=128: v4c cw7/9/11, v4, K2 132, CUDA combine (cmb_t[128]=1).
- cw is set through cap. Runs: 2 × 300 reps.
- Decide:
  - Pin a class's best arm at +0.5% or more in both replicates. These arms read the same bytes, so the sign transfers.
  - On a tie, apply R10. Pinning v4 at classes 8 and 16 ends the class-8 lottery and the measured 1.1% class-16 flip.
  - Change K2 or the combine only at +0.8% or more.

**DEC-06b — What is decode's err/bound tail on unseen seeds?** [30 · E2, E2b, DEC-02 V2 · no]
- Design, on the pinned bytes:
  - 13 cases × 300 seeds × 4 router draws;
  - the test-1 neighbourhood × 1000 seeds (B 1-4, ctx 1000-10000, full and sliding, q_len 1 and 8).
- Metric: p50, p99, max and the fitted P(>1). At the tightest case, force the attention arm (c320t, c320, sk, FA3) and the T=1 expert arm to attribute the error.
- Decide: p99 above 0.85, or any value above 0.95, opens a blocker-level margin task. The fix is to config-gate a more accurate arm or fix the kernel. Otherwise certify.
- The node runs the judge's SASS, so the numerics transfer.

**M03c — Which cold-half knob shortens the T=256 fork?** [10 · E1, E3 · carrier]
- Arms: H-16f with cold_bits[256] = cw<<4 for cw in {7, 9, 11}; cold v3; WG PDL off. Controls: H-16 and H-8f.
- nsys gives each half's time and the overlap.
- Decide: pin at +0.8% or more in both replicates, and only if the cold half is on the critical path.
- These knobs survive S1a.

**M08+P2 — Is the T≤8 expert cost fixed or per expert?** [26 · E1b, E3 · no]
- Design:
  - T=1 with k in {0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8} local experts; T=8 with k in {0, 2, ..., 14} at about 13 local routes.
  - Arms: v1, v1+pf4, v4, v3 at T=1; v1, v4, v4c at T=8.
  - Runs: 2 × 200 reps per k, plus nsys at 3 values of k each (K1, K2, combine, gaps).
- Metric: a fit of t = a + b·k.
- Decide:
  - a(T=1) of 12 us or more: S3 becomes fixed-cost work (fusion or a persistent T≤8 kernel), worth 1% or more of the headline.
  - b at 1.3× the ring rate (9.28 us per expert) or more, concentrated in one kernel: S3 becomes a streaming engine.
  - The arm with the smallest b is the class-1/8 pin candidate.
- a should transfer about 1:1, and b at ×0.69-0.97 (the judge measured 10.3 us per expert).

**DEC-02 — Is the decode node faithful to the judge?** [30 · E2, E2b, E3 · no]

- V2:
  - each test's err/bound falls inside the judge's envelope for that test index (at least 12/13; the 13th within 1.5× the range);

- V3:
  - shim kernels within ±1 us of the judge profile at T=1 and T=32 (the judge's norm_residual is 3.4-4.2 us per boundary);

- Decide:
  - V2 fails: stop decode work.
  - V3 fails: arms next to a shimmed op stay unresolved.
  - A GEMM or FA3 mismatch: arms that change that kernel need the judge.
