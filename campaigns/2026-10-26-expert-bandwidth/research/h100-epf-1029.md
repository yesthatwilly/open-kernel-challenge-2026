## Design (as built)

- **Launch:** grid 132 (1 CTA/SM), 320 threads: warps 0-7 consume, warp 8 produces, warp 9 runs the epilogue.
- **One ring for every record.** D 3 (D 4 tested); 194 KB of smem (231 KB at D 4); 128 registers.
- **Record order, expert-major and strided.**
  - K1: CTA c takes q = c + 132 i.
  - K2: the same, rotated so that the CTAs with one K1 record less take the extra K2 record.
  - So expert e's h is complete early, and the K2 records of e < last overlap the barrier on the last expert.
- **The h handoff:**
  - The epilogue bumps `cnt[e]` (fence.acq_rel + atomic) after its last K1 record of e.
  - It polls with relaxed loads plus a fence, using the non-blocking `mbarrier.test_wait` (with `try_wait` the poll
    latency was ~4-5 us).
  - It pulls h[e] (8 KB) into an smem slice and arms `hbar[e]`. Consumers wait on `hbar[e]` before their first K2
    record of e.
- **Combine:** the CTA that takes the last `done` ticket runs `ea6_cmb_kernel`'s body on warps 0-7, with its inputs
  preloaded, then resets the counters.

## Exactness

At n = 1..5 the output is **bitwise equal to master's chain F**:
- `out` matches over 4 eager launches (D3/D4, back to back) and after graph replay;
- h, the local y rows and `local_ids` are equal;
- the err words are 0 and the counters are back to 0 after each launch.

So the error vs master is 0, and the error vs fp32 equals master's.

## Verdict (pre-registered rule)

**EPF is bit-exact but 8.3% SLOWER on the T=1 expert block** (wW1 35.09 vs 32.17-32.18 us). In consume-only mode, the
judge's regime, it is also 8.3-8.5% slower.

- **Not a candidate.** EPF closes at T=1; no hyp.py triage was run.
- **The streaming slope does improve** (9.1 vs 10.65 us/expert, node HBM-bound).
- **That gain does not reach the judge.** In consume-only mode the slopes are equal (6.8 vs 6.85), so on the
  consume-coupled judge the fusion buys no throughput.
- **A fused T=1 cannot win by merging launches.** The fixed costs above (intercept 11 vs 4 us) outweigh it at the
  judge's n mix (n2-3).
- **What a future T=1 design would have to fix:** keep the K2 tail at master's 16 consumer warps/SM, and avoid the
  serial last-CTA combine and the per-expert barrier on the last expert.
