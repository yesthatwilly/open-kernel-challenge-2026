## 0. Pre-registration (from the brief)

- **Bar** (m256 reopen condition 1): a hot-half form that runs the dequant concurrently with wgmma without the slowdown,
  measured >= x1.4 per 128-k stage on the kstep rungs (consume-only, `[cyc]`), at an N where the hot half carries weight
  (N = 32..128; the judge's p50 hot tiles are 32 x5, 80, 96).
- **Decision:**
  - >= x1.4: write an integration plan (no submission edits);
  - x1.15-1.4: shelf note;
  - below x1.15: null.
- **Exactness:** every candidate form must be bit-identical to wh5ds on the same data.

## 1. Verdict

**NULL. No form reaches x1.15, let alone x1.4. The best exact new form is +1.8% at N32 and loses at every other N.**

The x1.4 bar is unreachable in two ways:
- **By the floor at N >= 96:** in consume-only, wh5ds is already at the 8N tensor floor at N128 (1024 cyc = 8 x 128), and
  its ceiling is x1.18 at N96 and x1.35 at N80.
- **By an interference law at N <= 64:** while the tensor core runs, every ALU-pipe and FMA-pipe instruction on that SM
  runs about 2x slower. This holds for RS and SS, and whether the dequant is in the MMA warp or in another warp. So the
  dequant cannot hide under the MMA.

| candidate (brief) | form built | best ratio vs wh5ds (consume-only, in-bench) | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| ping-pong consumer WGs, register / occupancy made to fit | **pp** (exact; consume-only, no ring) | **x1.018** at N32, x0.89-0.97 at N48-128 | the other WG's dequant slows ~2x under this WG's group (dx, dxa, dxf); more warps do not add dequant throughput (dq1/dqo/dq3) |
| dequant into smem in a layout one SS wgmma reads | **c2s / c2p / c2q** (exact: stmatrix into a SWIZZLE_128B bf16 tile, SS wgmma, serial / double-buffered / dequant-before-wait) | x0.60-0.79 | smem: the tensor's SS operand reads use the same 128 B/clk pipe (mss = 48 KB / 128 B/clk at N32). A in smem costs >= 4 B/el (write + read) on top of B, which puts a floor of >= ~700 cyc at N32 vs wh5ds 649 |
| K split, dequant ahead + deeper register ring | its ideal upper bound, **drs** (an independent dequant under an in-flight fixed-A 8-wgmma group, no register ring) | x1.074 at N32, <= x1.006 at N48-128 | the bound itself is below x1.15. The real form also needs 16 wgmma in flight, which ptxas serializes at 168 regs (hh4 C7512) |

No integration plan and no shelf entry. hh4/m256's "RF-port slowdown" inference now has a measured, general form; see
section 3.

### 2.2 The hhcc rung bench (consume-only by construction)

**Method:**
- 132 CTAs x 384 threads, `__launch_bounds__(384, 1)`, the same 168-register budget as wg_kernel. The third WG exits
  unless a rung uses it.
- 3 stages (codes + scales + B slabs in wh5ds's layout) stay resident in smem: no producer, no TMA, no mbarrier.
- clock64 is read around the K loop in every consumer warp. Cycles per stage = slope between KS 64 and 320.
- The dequant (A5b/x5), the descriptors (D) and wh5ds's two loops (SC for N < 48, R4 ring for N >= 48) are copied
  verbatim; only the stage wait and release are removed.
- **Replicates:** 3 processes x 3 interleaved rounds. Replicate spread is <= 10 cyc in every cell.
- **Exactness:** accumulators are compared bit for bit with wh's. With 3 stages and 2 A buffers every A-tile rewrite
  carries new values, so a race would show. "E" = 0 diffs over 132 x 256 x N/2 words.

The in-bench wh runs 10-20% below co5's kstep, because the harness also pays mbarrier waits and item and epilogue costs.

## 3. What this establishes [cyc]

1. **The consumer's ceiling per N.** In consume-only, MMA alone runs exactly at the 8N tensor floor for RS. SS runs at
   the 8N floor for N >= 64, but is smem-bound below that (386 at N32). The dequant alone is ~409. So the
   perfect-overlap ceiling vs wh5ds is:

   | N | ceiling | equals |
   |---|---|---|
   | 32 | x1.59 | 649 / 409 |
   | 48 | x1.62 | |
   | 64 | x1.43 | 731 / 512 |
   | 80 | x1.35 | |
   | 96 | x1.18 | |
   | 128 | **x1.00** | |

   x1.4 is possible in principle only at N <= 64, and only with complete hiding.
   - On co5's kstep basis the N128 bar (811) is below the tensor floor (1024).
   - wh5ds's N >= 96 stage time in the real kernel (1094-1159) is producer / HBM / fixed cost, not consumer slack.
2. **The interference law.** While wgmma runs on an SM, other instructions on it issue at about half rate:
   - ALU-pipe work, x1.7-2.2 slower;
   - FMA-pipe work, x2.0-2.3 slower;
   - the real dequant, x1.9-2.3 slower.

   It does not matter whether the work is in the warp that issued the wgmma (drs, dss) or in another warpgroup (dx, dxa,
   dxf), or whether the wgmma is RS or SS (dss is *worse* than drs because the dequant's LDS also competes for smem).
   This is the general form of hh4's "RF-port" inference (dov2, pp). The tensor's share of the SMSP register-file or
   dispatch bandwidth is the consistent reading, since it is not pipe-specific; it is still an inference, not a counter.
   **Consequence:** with ~409 cyc of dequant and 8N of MMA, every overlap form lands near dequant + MMA x ~0.85 (drs, pp),
   which is wh5ds's additive stage again.
3. **More warps or a second CTA cannot buy dequant throughput.** It saturates at 2 warps per SMSP (31.3 -> 32.5 rows /
   100 cyc from 2 to 3 warps). The 2-CTA / ping-pong idea therefore loses on both counts: no extra throughput, and the
   interference remains.
4. **Smem-routed A is excluded by bandwidth, not by layout.** mss shows the tensor's SS operand reads consume the
   128 B/clk smem pipe (N32: 32 KB A + 16 KB B in 386 cyc). Any A-in-smem form pays >= 4 B/el (STS + tensor read) plus
   B, codes and scales: >= ~89 KB per stage, >= ~700 cyc at N32, vs wh5ds 649. The measured exact forms c2s/c2p/c2q
   (x0.60-0.79) sit above even that. This confirms forensics D3 for the direct-to-SS layout too.

## 4. Reopen condition (replaces m256 condition 1)

The hot-half consumer can only get faster at N <= 64 by **cutting the dequant's instruction count**, because overlap
cannot hide it. That means fewer ALU + FMA ops per element than A5b's ~9 ops per code word + 4 HFMA2. A cut moves the
stage about 1:1 (wh ~ MMA + 0.85 x dequant). At N >= 96 there is no consumer slack.

Rough sizing: a 25% dequant cut is ~-90 cyc at N32 (~x1.16 on the stage). On the hot half that is ~1-2% of the moe
T=256 row, well under 3% headline. **Any future hot-half lever should be priced against `dqo` (409) and the 8N floor,
not against an overlap.**
