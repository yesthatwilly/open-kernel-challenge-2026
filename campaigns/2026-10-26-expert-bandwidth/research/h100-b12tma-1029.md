## Question (pre-registered, from the brief)

Why did the TMA-box b12 build (b12mc diag, check 5f35efd5: pooler m8 mc 72.8 / uc 72.7 / cp.async bridge 24.3 /
incumbent 17.9 us on the judge) run ~3x slower than the bridge, and can a fixed build reach <= the bridge?
Decision rule: fixed uc or mc <= bridge AND mc/uc <= 0.9 -> reopen b12mc as a judge diag and the decode bulk12 shelf;
fixed <= bridge but mc/uc > 0.9 -> the lever is TMA delivery, not multicast (report); can't reach the bridge -> record
why and close TMA-box b12 for m=8.

## Method

The probe copies the diag's device code verbatim (`b12mc_kernel` -> `b12t_kernel`, `b12x_kernel` = a7da6fa9's rotated
bridge) and adds template knobs; every arm reads the same synthetic 12-bit codes (tile layout of `_b12_encode`,
96 escapes per CTA, base 0x38), checked against an fp64 reference on the host-decoded true weights.

- Shapes: pooler m8 (N 4096, R 32, NT 8), pooler m16 (NT 16), qkv_gate m8 (N 2304, R 16, CW 4). K 8192.
- Arms: `v0*` = the judged build (2 CTAs/SM instances S6/S4 MINB2, cluster 4); `AR` = consumer release style:
  AR0 judged (lane 0 issues 4 serial `mbarrier.arrive.release.cluster.shared::cluster`, producer polls
  `try_wait.parity.acquire.cluster`), AR1 (release.cluster kept, lane q -> rank q in parallel), AR2 (CUTLASS
  `ClusterBarrier::arrive(cta_id)` style: plain `mbarrier.arrive.shared::cluster`, plain try_wait) -> `v1*`, `v2*`;
  `_c2` = cluster 2; `_s8` = S8 MINB1 (1 CTA/SM); `uc1` = TMA boxes with no cluster (local empty barrier);
  `GA` = escape-x gather (`_g1` batched loads, `_g2` batched + done right after the ring fill); `br` = bridge
  (pooler: a7da6fa9 inst 6/12, S8 MINB1; qkv: inst 8 `B12XQkv4` S4 MINB2); `cublas` = cuBLAS bf16 GEMM on the raw
  2-byte weights (a proxy for "a good bf16 kernel", NOT the judge's Triton incumbent, which needs torch/triton);
  `stream` = a pure read of the same 12-bit code bytes (the H100 floor for those bytes).
- Timing: 256 MB read flush before every launch, event pair per launch, empty-kernel baseline subtracted, 10 rounds x 41
  reps in rotating interleaved order; the rule reads the WORST round. Instr: per-CTA clock64/globaltimer/%smid stamps
  (separate INS builds). ncu 2026.2.1 (SpeedOfLight, Occupancy, WarpStateStats, MemoryWorkloadAnalysis(_Tables),
  SourceCounters with SASS) on every non-INS kernel. Effective SM clock under load 1.80-1.81 GHz.

## Root cause 1 (the 3x): the consumer's cluster-scope release is four serial MEMBAR.ALL.GPU per chunk

- **SASS** (`b12tma-sass-sum.txt`): each `mbarrier.arrive.release.cluster.shared::cluster` compiles to
  `MEMBAR.ALL.GPU` + `ERRBAR` + `SYNCS.ARRIVE.TRANS64.RED`; AR0 unrolls 4 of them on lane 0 (MEMBAR.ALL.GPU x6 /
  ERRBAR x6 per kernel vs x2 in AR2, the 2 left being the start/end `barrier.cluster`), and the `acquire.cluster`
  try_wait adds `CCTL.IVALL` (an L1 invalidate) on the producer's poll path.
- **Instr** (cycles per chunk, warp 1 / producer lane): v0 cons_arrive **4,020-4,125** (pooler m8/m16, qkv), prod_wait
  3,200-3,800 (the producer waits on those arrivals); v1 (parallel, fence kept) 1,330-1,360; v2 **27-52**; bridge 3-33.
  v0's consumer loop is 4,690 cycles/chunk = 2.6 us/chunk, constant across shapes (the judge's 72.8 us / 32 chunks =
  2.27 us/chunk and 35.3 / 16 = 2.2 us/chunk at qkv had the same signature).
- **ncu** pc-sampled stalls: v0 membar 61-74% (the four ERRBAR lines hold 56% of pooler m8's samples, the four arrives
  18%); v1 membar 35-52%; v2 / bridge / uc1 0%. DRAM throughput 16% (v0) -> 31% (v1) -> 45-51% (v2, uc1, bridge 49%).
- Ruled out: **co-residency** at the judged instances: MaxActiveClusters 62 >= 32 / 36 (H100 132 SMs = judge's H200
  SXM count; same numbers as the judge's occupancy line) and 0 spill. **But** 4-CTA clusters land on only 120 SMs
  (8 SMs double up, %smid census) and the spec's 1 CTA/SM S8 pair fits only 30 clusters < 32 (2 waves: v2*_s8 49-51 us);
  cluster 2 fixes placement (MaxActiveClusters 66 at 1/SM, 128 SMs used). **Bank conflicts** on the 12-bit unpack /
  swizzled ldmatrix: 18-22k conflicts per ~575k shared-load wavefronts (3%), the same in the bridge, not a factor.
  **TMA issue / barrier structure**: 1 UBLKCP + 4 (pooler) / 8 (qkv) UTMALDG.2D per chunk (multicast: 1-2 per rank),
  one full barrier per ring slot (no single expect_tx serialization), producer issue 270-520 cycles/chunk, never the
  bound.

## Root cause 2 (both families, the bigger lever): the escape-x gather is a serial tail

Warp 0 gathers `x[t][k_e]` for the CTA's escapes only after the whole stream is issued, and the gather's per-element
branches expose each global load one after another (24 loads per lane at m8, 48 at m16). Phase stamps (mean us from CTA
start, pooler m8): bridge consumer loop ends 18.6, gather ends **28.5**, CTA ends 29.2; uc1 17.1 / **26.5** / 27.2. The 8
consumer warps sit at the post-loop `__syncthreads` meanwhile (ncu: 28-30% of all warp samples on the BSSY after that
BAR.SYNC, bridge and TMA alike). Cost: ~10 us per 8 tokens (pooler m8 10.4, m16 20.6, qkv m8 10.4 us).
This matches the judge's unexplained "~1 us per token row" b12x residual (0acdca48: pooler m8/16/32 25.3 / 34.9 / 52.8 us,
about +9 us per 8 tokens), which FINDINGS read as L2 hot-spotting. Master's m=1 `b12_kernel` keeps x resident in smem and
reads escapes from there, so it does not have this tail; the b12x family (b12x checks, b12mc) does.
GA1 (eight independent loads per batch, same place) cuts the gather to ~4.4 us at m8; GA2 (batched, right after the
ring fill) finishes it at ~7 us from CTA start, overlapped, at the price of a short stream stall (it pays at m16).

## Verdict (pre-registered rule)

**Fixed build <= bridge, but mc/uc > 0.9 -> the lever is TMA delivery, not multicast.** Do not reopen b12mc multicast.
- The 3x was one defect: the cluster-scope release arrives (`.release.cluster` -> MEMBAR.ALL.GPU + ERRBAR, four in
  series per consumer warp per chunk, ~1,000 cycles each). The CUTLASS-style plain `mbarrier.arrive.shared::cluster`
  (one lane per peer) removes it: 94 -> 31 us (pooler m8). It hit uc as much as mc, which is why the judge's mc/uc read
  1.00 on a stalled kernel.
- The best TMA-box arm needs no cluster at all: `uc1` = 0.952 / 0.887 / 0.922 of the judged bridge (worst round), and
  0.958 / 0.926 / 0.883 of the gather-fixed bridge. Clusters cost placement (cluster 4 runs on 120 SMs) and coupling
  for no multicast return (x is 128-256 KB, ~1/4-1/3 of the code bytes per CTA, and comes from L2).
- **Bigger, family-wide:** the escape-x gather tail is 25-38% of every b12x kernel at m >= 8 (bridge 29.2 -> 21.7 us at
  pooler m8, 44.0 -> 27.4 at m16, 22.9 -> 17.6 at qkv m8 with batched loads). With it fixed, TMA-box uc1 at pooler m8
  (20.7 us) beats this node's cuBLAS bf16 (26.1) by 0.79x, at m16 0.96x, at qkv m8 0.92x, and sits at 1.22x / 1.50x /
  1.56x of the stream floor for its bytes.

## What transfers to the judge (H200 SXM, 4.8 TB/s)

- `[cyc]` transfers: the MEMBAR.ALL.GPU fence cost and its fix (the judge's 3.0x reproduces as 3.2x here), the gather
  tail (serial load latency, ~10 us per 8 tokens; the judge's b12x m8 -> m16 -> m32 steps show the same slope), the
  co-residency numbers (132 SMs both; MaxActiveClusters printed equal), mc/uc ~ 1 (x comes from L2 either way).
- `[hbm]` does not: absolute times and GB/s. On the judge the streaming part is ~30% shorter, so the latency-bound tail
  is a LARGER share there. Rough judge projection (assumes the H100 absolute savings carry over; untested): pooler m8
  bridge 24.3 -> ~16.8 us with the gather fixed (incumbent 17.9: ~0.94), uc1-style TMA boxes ~0.95 of that (~16 us,
  ~0.89 of the incumbent); pooler m16 b12x 34.9 (0acdca48) -> ~18 us vs incumbent ~18.1.

   m16 / qkv m8 under the read flush vs kernel_fn. The probe's `esc_gather<..., true>` and the `GA` producer-warp

   chunk), or a 10th warp; the GA2 arm still pays ~1-3 us of stream stall at m8.
3. Decode bulk12 shelf (CLAIMS:97): this result does not reopen it on multicast; it applies only where those GEMMs would
   use the b12x m>=8 structure (escape gather after the stream).
