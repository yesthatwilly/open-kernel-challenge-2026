## 0. Pre-registration (from the brief)

**Question.** With wh5ds (faster hot half) and E4/E5 (faster cold half) both landed, is H=-16 (hot = experts with more
than 16 routes, block 16) still the best split at T=256 (moe and decode) and T=128 (decode)?
**Decision rule.** An H at <= 0.985 of H-16 in **every** round is a retune candidate; otherwise H-16 stays, and the
curve is recorded.

## 1. Method

- nodejudge **arms** (R3a): one process and one prepare per run. The base submission routes as judged: moe gets the
  W6-M fork, wh / wh5ds and the HMX cold twin; decode gets HC_PIN H-16, wh5ds and the v4cxs cold twin. Four arms then
  force H on the **same prepared state** by copy-on-write `post`:
  - `hm.on.256` (moe, with `hm.fon.256 = true`, forked as judged);
  - `hc.on.128` / `hc.on.256` (decode; serial, as routed: `fk` None).
  - The arms are h16 (null against the routed base), h24, h12 and h08.
  - H-24 and H-12 are new codes. They are added for the arm's capture only, through `cap`:
    `HM_WG_HOT` / `HC_WG_HOT` += {-24: 24, -12: 12}.
  - H-24 also gets `HM_COLD_SLOTS` / `HC_COLD_SLOTS` = 32 (+ `HC_COLD_CAP`), so a cold expert with 17-24 routes fits
    the align buffers.
  - Every other route (wh kernel, cold twin, Engine A) stays as the base's prepare picked it under H-16.
- Exactness: each arm passed the eager reference check per shape, all TASK tests, and every timed replay
  (`check_every` 1). All parties `passed=True` in all 4 runs. The moe arms' rel-L2 matched the base to 4 digits.
- Timing: fresh judge-like inputs every repetition, an L2 read flush and graph replays. The order rotates across the
  5 parties. Each run does 20 reps = **10 judge-pair rounds**. A round's ratio is sqrt(r_2i r_2i+1) with r = t_arm / t_h16.
  The per-round script is `scratchpad/rounds.py`. It reads the raw `runs_ns`, so the ~3.8 us replay overhead is not
  subtracted, which biases ratios slightly toward 1.
- moe: master 09b11a9d (board 2.020 bytes), verify 32 = T=256. On the node, master's own wh race picked **wh**, not
  wh5ds (wh5ds/wh .979/.982/... against the 0.98 bar). The wh5ds verdict therefore comes from **hs-moe5**: the same
  bytes with `WH_5VSWH` 0.98 -> 9.0, which routed wh5ds (copy PASS). hs-moe-r1 (wh) is kept as a second curve.
- decode: branch `hyp/muse_decode_layer/engine-a-encodings-wh5ds-decode-stack` (e1a44258). Check 824b60fa was still
  running at 23:10Z. On the node the branch routed wh5ds at T=128 and T=256 and **no E** (`[ea-enc] PATH CHOSEN ...
  master (iso E/m > 0.98 in a round)` in both runs). That is the brief's fallback configuration (decode master + wh5ds),
  so the cold half here is the v4cxs twin, not E4/E5. Inputs: problems/decode-jl (judge-like router search). Rows
  verify 16 (T=128) and verify 32 (T=256), sliding and full layers. Two replicates (seeds 2026102911/12).

## 2. Verdict

**H-16 stays, for moe T=256 and for decode T=128 and T=256. No H is <= 0.985 of H-16 in every round of any row; no
H reaches it in even a majority of rounds of a replicated row. Not a retune candidate (no hyp.py triage needed).**

## 4. Caveats

- Decode measured **without** E4/E5 in the cold half: E did not route on the node. If the judge routes E (824b60fa),
  the cold half gets ~1% faster (iso 0.989-0.994 here). That would favour a larger hot set (H-12 / H-8) only by that
  margin, under the 1.5% the rule needs. Re-race only if E routes on the judge and this lane's T=256 lean is wanted
  settled.
- Arms reuse the H-16 prepare's other picks (cold twin, wh kernel, cold_bits). A retuned H would re-race them. That
  matters only for a candidate, and there is none.
- H100 vs the judge's H200: the hot/cold balance depends on HBM rate, so the absolute us do not transfer. These are
  same-GPU ratios.
