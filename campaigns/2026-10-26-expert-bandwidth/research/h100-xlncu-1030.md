The rest are in `analysis/xdelta.txt`: every class and kernel shows the same shape.

L's loop cut is real: 150-365 warp-cycles per record. But 45-100% of it comes back as barrier waits:
- **Fast warps cannot run ahead.** With D = 2 and one full/empty pair per 36,864 B stage, all 8 consumer warps of a CTA
  must release slot s before any of them can consume record i+2. Warps that finish sooner sit in `try_wait`, and at
  stream-K split points in `bar_consumers`, until the slowest warp of the stage catches up.
- **The dequant burst is ALU-pipe co-bound.** Most of what L removed was IMAD / IADD / ISETP / branch issue. That buys
  issue slots, but the ALU pipe stays the limit during the burst.

**Why W is slower** (xw-ncu, `analysis/kmetrics.txt`). ptxas emitted exactly the intended SASS: 32 IMAD.WIDE.U32

- FMA-heavy pipe utilisation rose 41→51% (W2: 58%) while FMA-heavy *instructions* per cycle rose only 0.815→0.885. So

## 5. Verdict (pre-registered rule)

**Null. No named change clears 4% consume-only at a class carrying ≥ 25% of the expert block.**
- The only candidate predicted near 4% was the ALU→FMA rebalance (W). Its prototype is exact but **2-8% slower (W) and
  10-17% slower (W2)** on every class, in all 3 replicates.
- Every other change is ≤ ~1-3% predicted, or does not fit in smem.

**The reason:**
- Inside the dequant burst, XL's consume step sits at its pipe floor. Per step that is 17.2-17.7 issued, 17.8-18.4
  ALU-pipe cycles and 13-15 FMA-heavy cycles. The dequant (code views + scale + HMUL2) is 85% of the record's
  instructions, and its op count is at the proven minimum.
- The remaining time is CTA coupling: full-barrier and run-end barrier waits, 12-30% of consumer warp time. The 2-slot
  ring and the per-unit shared x rows cannot be loosened within 2 × 113 KB of smem.
- That coupling is also where half of L's cut went.

**Consequences:**
- No `campaign/xl2` branch and no `.worktrees/xl2` were made.
- Master and okc were not touched.
- E4XL vs E5XL routing per class should stay with the judge's race. In consume-only the two are within 0.5-1.5% of each
  other, with E5 ALU-bound and E4 issue/ALU co-bound.
