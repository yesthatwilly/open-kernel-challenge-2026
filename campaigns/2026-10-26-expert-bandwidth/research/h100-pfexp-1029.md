## Question and decision rule (pre-registered by the caller)

Can a full-prefill attention kernel that runs a fraction f of its exp2 on the FMA pipe beat master's FA3 call by >= 10% at
the judge's full-layer prefill shapes, with the output inside tolerance? The rule: >= 10% at both turn_1 full and turn_4
full -> a prefill check candidate. 3-10% -> report and shelve. < 3% -> close the exp-split lever and say what bounds it.

## What was built

- Source: the FA3 that sglang's AOT CMakeLists pins, sgl-flash-attn (ex sgl-attn) `141fa063` plus cutlass `57e3cfb4`
  (its URL_HASH matched). The build used sgl's CUDA flags with an FP8-hdim64-only feature trim. BF16 was stubbed.
- `probes/pfexp/fetch-src.sh` fetches the source and `patch_softmax.py` patches it.
- Patch: in `softmax.h scale_apply_exp2`, on the FP8 path only, column ni of the thread's 40 score columns per row
  (kBlockN 160) takes `exp2_poly_fma` when `(ni*K) % 40 < K`, so f = K/40.
  - `exp2_poly_fma` is FA4's Cody-Waite split: fmax -127, add.rm against 1.5*2^23, then a degree-2 minimax polynomial
    (rel err 1.7e-3), with the floor integer-added into the exponent field.
  - Each variant is its own .so (`torch.ops.fa3x_k<K>.fwd`), and all of them load into one process next to sgl_kernel.
- SASS (k20 vs k0, same kernel): MUFU.EX2 164 -> 84, +480 instructions. **6 extra issue slots per exp moved**:
  FFMA +2, FADD +3, FMNMX +1, the rest LEA/IMAD.
- Timing: CUDA graphs with a cold L2 before each replay, 7 interleaved shuffled rounds x 20 reps. Inputs are pft1's:
  coherent FP8 keys, fixed seed. Master is sgl_kernel `flash_attn_with_kvcache` in contiguous mode B, timed alone and
  with its gather (+g).

## Verdict (per the pre-registered rule)

**Not a prefill check candidate.** Against master's FA3 every variant loses: 0.67-0.85x at t1f and t4f. Under the rule,
that is the "< 3%" branch.

**What bounds it is the base, not the lever.** The FA3 built from the pinned source is 1.31-1.34x slower than the
sgl_kernel 0.4.9 binary, and its output differs from master's (rel 5e-3 to 9e-3, with a slightly *lower* FP32 error).
So the wheel's kernel is not this source/flag/toolchain combination. Within the matched build, the exp split behaves as
the issue model predicts:
- It gains 8-12% at f = 0.10-0.20: t1f best at f 0.2 (1.10x), t4f best at f 0.1 (1.10-1.12x).
- Beyond f 0.3 it loses, because each moved exp costs 6 issue slots. Issue saturates first, which matches pft1's
  "realistic 1.2-1.3x" being optimistic.

**Lever status: shelved, not closed.** The size if it did carry over onto master's kernel:
- About 10% of FA3 main is about 4% of the t1f row and 4.3% of the t4f row.
- Weighted by the triage shares (t1f 11.4%, t4f 12.7%, first-turn full rows about 10.7%), that is **about 1-1.5%
  headline**. That is below /hypothesize's 2.5% bar even before the base gap is solved.

Reopen only with an FA3 build that matches the wheel's speed and bytes. Check the wheel's own build provenance
(compiler, flags, commit) first.
