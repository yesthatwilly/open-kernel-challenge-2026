# H100 lane: UFS stage 1, the fused dependency-aware cold engine for moe verify 32 (bs 256), 2026-10-30 (gpunode06 GPU 4, 03:01Z-03:22Z)

**Verdict: NULL.**
- The fused cold path is bit-exact, and it never hung or timed out.
- In consume-only mode, the mode that predicts the judge, it is **slower** than master's cold pair in all 3 replicates:
  - E4XL x1.027-1.041 (+3.8..+5.9 us);
  - E5XL, master's judge arm, x1.069-1.087 (+9.3..+12.1 us).
- Normal mode reads -0.7..-1.7 us (x0.992-0.997), but that is behind the H100 HBM wall.
- The judge-scaled saving is **<= 0 us** against a pre-registered bar of >= 8 us. The diagnostic upper bound (fused, no
  dependency waits, which is unsafe) is x0.984-0.999, ~-0.2..-2 us on the node. **No stage-2 plan**, no moe branch, no submission change.

## Brief and rule (pre-registered)

- Fuse the T=256 cold half's Engine A K1 (w13) and K2 (w2) launches (master c5e2d8d4: H-16 forked hybrid, cold v4cxs +
  E4XL/E5XL from the `'4xl'` module) into one persistent kernel with per-expert w13 -> w2 readiness counters, one
  build_map and one fix-up round, reusing the XL consumer as-is.
- Measure it against master's cold path on judge-shaped T=256 draws, normal and consume-only, in 3 replicates, bit-exact.
- **Decision:** a cut of >= 8 us judge-scaled (>= ~0.5% headline), exact -> write a stage-2 plan; otherwise a null.
- Coordinator update mid-lane (ufsrung, research/h100-ufsrung-1030.md): there are only ~11-12 us of UFS-fillable idle,
  and the w13 -> w2 hand-off has no drain. Any gain must therefore come from the busy fixed-cost cut (one build_map, fill
  and fix-up; ufsrung guessed -8..-12 us), so measure that directly. **This lane measures it, and it is ~0.**

## Code

- takemyjob branch **`hopper/ufs-1030` b349935** (worktree `~/git/.takemyjob-wt/ufs`, off `hopper/xlres-1030`),
  `prototypes/hopper-probe/probes/ufs/`:
  - `patch_ufs.py` patches xlres's bench snapshot `xr-1313d7761298.py`. That is eaenc2's bench harness, and its XL loop
    is the text master's `'4xl'` carries.
  - `ufsb.py` is the T=256 cold-half bench; it replaces `_encb`.
  - `mkcfg.py` and `repack.sh` build the bundles `njufs0` (the baseline, the same bytes as `njxlres`) and `njufs1..3`
    (UFS).
  - Configs `ufs-r1..r3` (xr-r1 with another snapshot and seed). Snapshots `ufsc{1,2,3}-*.py` are untracked; rebuild them
    with `patch_ufs.py <xr snap> <out> <seed>`.
- **`ea4x_ufs<DQ>`** (DQ 27 = E4XL, 28 = E5XL):
  - It is one launch at grid 264, 2 CTAs/SM. It runs the barrier init and **one** `build_map_x` (plus the w2 gs into 128
    more bytes of smem: 113,264 B), then `griddep_launch`.
  - Each CTA then runs its K1 stream-K range and then its K2 range. These are **the exact splits of the separate
    kernels at the same grid**, so the contributor sets and the fp32 order are unchanged, and h / y are bitwise.
  - Both phases share one ring, with slot and parity carried across the phase boundary. The K2 producer prefetches K2
    weights while the consumers finish K1.
  - `ufs_phase` is `expert_body_x`'s text with targeted edits only: ring offset, producer, a readiness signal after the
    K1 epilogue. The XL consumer loop is unchanged.
- **Dependencies:** a counter per (pass, kc), 64 = 8 K1 super-groups x 8 consumer warps.
  - **Signal:** each consumer warp that runs a K1 epilogue does a proxy fence, then `__syncwarp`, then a release fence,
    then `atomicAdd`.
  - **Wait:** the K2 producer, on entering a pass, waits on its 8 counters with lanes 0..7 in parallel. The spin is
    bounded at 2^27 cycles, after which it sets err[1] and skips further waits. It uses `ld.acquire.gpu`, then
    `fence.proxy.async.global`, before the TMA x loads.
- **Deadlock safety:**
  - Every CTA runs all its K1 units before any K2 unit, and K1 never waits on anything K2 does.
  - The non-DEFER fix-up only takes tickets. The grid is fully resident.
  - The last producer to exit resets the counters.
  - Canary: an eager first launch with a flushed print before and after `synchronize`.
- **Diagnostic arms** (timing only):
  - `u1` = the fused kernel's K1 phase alone;
  - `uw` = fused **without** dependency waits (racy; it bounds what fusion alone can give);
  - `ub` = K1 signals batched once per CTA.
- Registers: K1/K2 XL 94/92, UFS 96/96. lmem 0, occupancy 2 for every kernel; no C7512 (no wgmma in this kernel).

**Diagnostics (v3, consume-only, all 3 replicates):**
- **u1 / k1 = 1.029-1.046** on all 36 draw x arm cells (mean 1.037-1.039 in every replicate): the fused kernel's K1 phase alone is ~3.5-4 us slower than the
  separate K1. The XL loop is the same text, but the kernel carries two instantiated XL loops (2x the code: likely
  i-cache) at 96 vs 92-94 registers.
- **uw / sep = 0.970-1.012**; per replicate the means are E4XL .994 / .986 / .984 and E5XL .999 / .993 / .993, i.e.
  ~-0.2..-2 us. Even with the dependency removed, which is unsafe, fusion gains ~1-2 us net. The real fixed cost saved (the second launch, build_map, ring fill and PDL hand-off) is therefore ~4-5 us
  on the node: it only just pays back the u1 penalty.
- **The waits cost +6..+12 us** (ufs vs uw). A CTA's K2 now waits on its own band's slowest K1 CTA with a static K2
  binding. The separate pair re-places K2 CTAs dynamically on whichever SM frees first, and it has already filled their
  rings under PDL.
- ub (batched signals) ~ ufs, so the signal fences are not the cost. v2 batched them and lost the normal-mode gain
  (v1 n -3.7 / -4.0 us, v2 -0.5 / -0.8).

Normal mode is at the HBM wall (sep 177-262 us vs c 114-169), so its -0.7..-1.7 us (v1 -3.7..-4.0) does not transfer;
the campaign's transfer key uses consume-only ratios only.

## Judge-scaled saving and verdict

- With the consume-only ratio as the predictor and XL's observed ~0.5-0.6x transfer, judge-scaled is **+2..+6% slower**
  on the cold half. Taking only the clock factor (x0.924) on the node delta gives **+5..+11 us**. Either way, the saving
  is **<= 0 us**.
- The diagnostic ceiling (uw, no dependency) is ~-0.2..-2 us node, <= ~-1..-2 us judge-scaled. Even with the u1 code
  penalty also removed (~-4.5 us more), it would be ~-5..-7 us node, ~-3..-6 us judge-scaled, still below the 8 us
  bar. Reaching that would also
  need the dependency to be free, which it is not.
- **NULL** under the pre-registered rule. No stage-2 plan, no `.worktrees/ufs` / `campaign/ufs`, and moe master is
  untouched.
- This agrees with ufsrung: the cold half's "fixed cost" (27.4 us in the m256 fit) is mostly not removable launch or
  build_map overhead. The w13 -> w2 hand-off is already packed by PDL plus dynamic CTA placement, and fusing it trades
  that placement for a static band dependency.

Reopen only with a different form: a dynamic work queue (atomic unit counter, no static K2 binding) **and** a
single-XL-loop kernel (one instantiation, W13 as a runtime flag, to recover u1's 4%). Before that is built, its
consume-only ceiling has to be shown >= 8 us judge-scaled; this lane's uw bound says that is unlikely.
