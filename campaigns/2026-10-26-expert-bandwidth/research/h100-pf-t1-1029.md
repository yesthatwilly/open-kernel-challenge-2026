## Verdict (per the pre-registered rule)

**No node-measurable lever >= 10% on any non-rega turn_1 bucket. No prefill check candidate from this lane.**

- Dense BF16 GEMMs (the largest non-rega bucket, 7.2% + 2.5% headline): at cuBLAS's own ceiling (650-820 TF/s);
  backend and Triton tiles lose. Residual gap vs the best-tile rate: qkv ~2 us, o ~4 us per turn_1 shape (< 0.3% headline).
- FA3 full (4.8% headline at turn_1, 13.6% over all full rows): MUFU-bound at 70% XU; 1.51x its pure-exp floor; no knob
  moves it; f16x2 exps do not raise the exp rate.
- Sliding attention (1.1% headline at turn_1): knobs +/-0.5%; a contiguous window (+7.7%) is eaten by its gather (0.97).
- norm_residual boundaries: 12 us HBM gap per shape (0.4% headline), HBM-bound (H100 does not measure it).
