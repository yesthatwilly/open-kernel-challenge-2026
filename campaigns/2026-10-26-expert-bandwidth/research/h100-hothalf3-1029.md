## 0. Pre-registration (from the brief; written after the build + one quick validation pass, before the full run)

**Question.** Does A5b+D+SC (rega2) add on top of wh (A3 + exact N + R4 ring wait<2> for N >= 48) in the wg hot half?

**Decision rule.** Forked end-to-end vs wh (mean over dec a/b/c), at T=256 or T=128:
- <= 0.95 -> a follow-up decode check stacking it on wh (expected decode + moe headline via hh2's elasticities);
- 0.95-0.98 -> rides with wh's next revision;
- > 0.98 -> the hot half's N <= 64 chain needs something else (say what ncu shows).

Exactness: every part must be bit-identical to master (dq0) on w13 and w2 in all 8 routings.

## 1. Verdict (rule 0)

**Band > 0.98. The rega2 parts add only ~1.2-1.3% forked on top of wh, so no follow-up decode check. The N <= 64 chain
needs something else: more warps or more ILP per SMSP, not fewer instructions (section 4).**

- **Forked end-to-end vs wh** (paired per round, mean over dec a/b/c, 20 rounds per cell):
  - wh5ds (the pre-registered A5b+D+SC on wh): **x0.987 at T=256, x0.988 at T=128**.
  - The best variants: h5ds80 x0.987 / x0.986, x5ds x0.990 / x0.984. Round spread <= +-0.2%.
  - No cell reaches 0.98.
- **Isolated pair vs wh:** wh5ds x0.973 at T=256, x0.967 at T=128. h5ds80 x0.972 / x0.942, x5ds x0.979 / x0.946.
  - The parts cut the hot pair 3-6%. Forked, about half of that lands: at T=128 the cold half (2x the hot) hides
    most of the cut, and at T=256 the hot share of the end is ~half.
- **Headline** (hh2's elasticities):
  - Decode, forked: wh +1.32% -> wh5ds **+1.53%** (+0.21% on top of wh); h5ds80 / x5ds +1.55-1.56%.
  - moe (jd-T256 forked, A3-only per hh2): A3 +0.95%, wh +0.92%, wh5ds / x5ds **+1.29-1.30%** (vs wh x0.982, -5.9 us).
- **Still worth carrying.** Every part is bit-exact at 168 regs with no spill. If wh's next revision touches the bank prepare
  anyway, A5b+D is a free +0.2% (decode) / +0.35% (moe). It is not worth a check of its own.

What ncu says about N <= 64:

- **It is latency-bound, not issue- or pipe-bound.**
  - Issue slots are 37-45% busy. The ALU pipe is 36-41% (rega's pure dequant was ALU-bound at 68-71%). The tensor
    pipe is 26-49%.
  - Each SMSP has ~2.3 active warps and 0.6 eligible: the 2 consumer warps plus the producer.
  - Each warp issues once every 4.6-5.5 cycles. The top stall is `wait` (fixed-latency dependency, ~1.1 per issue),
    then long scoreboard and the wgmma/barrier waits.
- **A5b+D removes 10-17% of the issued instructions, but the issue rate drops with them** (45 -> 40%).
  - A shorter dependent chain does not shrink, so cycles fall only 5-14%.
- **The lever for the N <= 64 chain is more independent work per SMSP:**
  - more consumer warps: a third consumer warpgroup, or the 64 rows split across more warps;
  - or more ILP per warp: two items' or two k16 steps' dequant chains interleaved, since the ring's one step in
    flight is not enough at N <= 64.
  - The register budget (168 regs, N128 at C7512 in rgx2) is the constraint. At N <= 64 the accumulator is only 16-32
    regs, so a second, independent dequant chain or a 3-WG N <= 64 instantiation has room.
  - Cutting more dequant instructions will not help: rgx2's exact-cover search puts 9 ops/word at the floor, and the
    issue slots are not the limit.

## 5. What transfers to the H200 judge

- **Transfers [cyc]:**
  - The isolated pair ratios, the K-step table, the SASS counts and the ncu stall structure (same GH100 SM, 132 SMs,
    consumer-bound).
  - The bank re-encodings are prepare-time and lossless; their range precondition is A3's (scale bytes in [0x08, 0x7E]).
- **Does not transfer:**
  - Absolute us and the bw arm [hbm].
  - The forked ratios are a judge-shaped model: the cold stand-in has R from judge anatomy, but no ALU or L2 contention.
    On the H200 the cold half is relatively faster, so a forked hot cut may land a little more fully. That does not move
    a x0.987 near 0.95.
- **Not covered:**
  - The real ea4x cold kernels and the judge's exact per-expert counts (a/b/c bracket the shape).
  - ncu ran on whole kernels (producer warp and epilogue included), not on the consumer loop alone.
