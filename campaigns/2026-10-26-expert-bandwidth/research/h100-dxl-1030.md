## 1. The t1xl port (what changed in `[ea-enc]`)

- `_eae_src6`: moe t1xl's trimmed record loop (`_EAE_T1XL`) as ea6 template `int LT`, with the dispatch
  `if (flags & 1024) fn = ... <2, true, EQ, 1>` and ea6_info rows 5..8. The loop calls decode's **generated**
  `consume_lt<EQ, 1, 1>` (from `_EAE_CONSUME`, shared with '4xl' through a new `_eae_lts()`; '4xl's text is
  unchanged) instead of moe's separate hand-written `consume_lt<DQ>` text: same loads, order, ldmatrix.x4 B preload
  and mma per step (the NT 2 / XB 0 branches are `if constexpr`-dead), which saves ~3.3 KB. The P handoff store is
  inlined, and the loop's `dme` timer lines are dropped (decode always passes an empty dbg tensor, so `dme` is null).
  Registers on cu13.0 (node): K1 E4XL **72**, E5XL 64, K2 72 / 64, lmem 0, occupancy 1 / 2 = the E rows (moe: the
  same).
- `_ead6_forward_core`: enc digits map through `EAE_ARMS` (5 / 6 = XL on bank copy 3 / 4) and set flag 1024.
- `EAE_T1 = ("33", "44", "30", "53", "55", "64", "66")`; admission of an XL digit needs ea6e row 5+i ok, lmem 0 and
  occupancy >= the E row's; `_eae_info` returns every row.
- Selection: "XL arm" is any arm containing 5 / 6, so the existing tie (an XL arm replaces the best passing E arm
  only at XL/E median < `EAE_XL_TIE` 0.995) and the layer veto cover the T=1 pairs. The bar (0.98 every round), veto
  (layer f, every round <= 1.0), copy check and every other class are unchanged. PATH CHOSEN no longer says "(no XL
  arm: ea6)".
- **Every other module's CUDA text is byte-identical to t256xl**: every top-level string constant is equal (except
  the removed `_EA5_CPP` and the two new ones), and `_ea4x_source`, `_ea6_source`, `_eae_src4x`, `_eae_src4xl`,
  `_wh_src` generate identical text (checked by evaluating them from both files). Only `_eae_src6` differs.

## 4. Sizing on the judge

The headline is the geomean of 12 rows (6 loads x sliding / full). The T=1 rows are decode 1 sliding / full at 90.4 /
94.5 us (beaed2ad). Their expert block is K1 10.0 / 14.8 us + K2 7.3 / 12.1 us (co-resident) + cmb 2.0 / 2.9 us,
so its wall is roughly 18 / 25 us.

**Does the worst T=1 round get under 0.98?** Decode's judge T=1 iso (E/m per round, 2 draws x 5 rounds):

| run | E4/E4 | E5/E5 | routed |
|---|---|---|---|
| beaed2ad (scored, 1.7196) | 0.971 [0.961, 0.985] | 0.971 [0.958, 0.984] | master |
| e9c076e5 (check, 1.7285) | 0.978 [0.968, 0.991] | 0.976 [0.961, 0.994] | master |
| 7c7524a6 (scored, 1.7225 board) | - | passed | **E5/E5** |

- XL/E transfer: on the node, consume-only XL/E per round at n2-4 is 1.022-1.029 (x 0.6 judge elasticity, t1b) ->
  **judge XL/E ~0.983-0.987 per round**. moe confirms the direction on the judge: before XL its T=1 never routed (worst
  0.981 / 0.988 / 0.989 in 24f183ff / 575820c9 / 922ddf4c); with XL it routed in both runs (check E4XL/E4XL, scored
  E5XL/E5).
- Applied to decode: E4XL/E4XL median ~0.963-0.965, worst ~0.977 (e9c076e5's rounds) and ~0.971 (beaed2ad's).
  **The worst round goes under 0.98 in both reference runs**, by a margin of ~0.003-0.009. With the per-round sd ~0.0075
  (range / 3.08 over 10 rounds), P(some round > 0.98) is ~0.1-0.2 per run, against ~2/3 of runs failing today (two of
  the three runs above).
- **The layer veto** (`_eae_veto`: full layer f mode, every round <= 1.0) is not the "E4/m 0.994 [0.968, 1.013]"
  number. That figure is the iso of arm 30 (K1 E4 with master's K2), which is a race arm, not the veto, and never the
  winner. E5/E5 already passed the real veto in 7c7524a6. An XL pair is faster than its E pair, so it passes too.
  (On the node the veto reads 1.007 / 1.007 / 1.001 / .999 for E4XL/E4XL because of the HBM wall, which is why the
  node routes master.)

**Gain:**
- XL route vs master: ~3.6% of the block (iso ~0.964) = ~0.65 / 0.9 us on the two decode 1 rows (0.72% / 0.95%) ->
  **+0.14% headline**.
- XL vs the E route: ~1.2-1.5% of the block = ~0.25 / 0.3 us -> +0.05%.
- Expected vs today's routing lottery (T=1 routes E in ~1 of 3 runs: ~0.03% expected) -> **~+0.1% headline (range
  +0.05-0.15%)**. That is a rider, far below the 1.24% one-run margin and below tonight's ~1.7% check-vs-scored drift.

**ms_align, if it had room:** the judge's Triton align is 9.6 us at T=256 and 5.4 us at T=128. CUDA is ~3.0 us at
T=256 (moe judge 2.98) and ~2.6-2.8 us at T=128. The node shows the T=256 layer saving the whole iso delta through the
fork, and T=128 about half of it. Rows: verify 32 405.2 / 681.8 us at d ~6.6 us; verify 16 319.7 / 452.3 us at
d ~1.4 us. That is **~+0.28% headline (range +0.2-0.35%)**, about 3x t1xl.

## 5. Not done / next

- **ms_align on decode needs a room decision.** It is bit-identical and on the T=256 critical path (+0.2-0.35%), but a
  minimal port needs ~7.4 KB: `_MS_CPP` + `_MS_SRC` verbatim is 5,286 B, the wrapper ~0.9 KB, and the build thread,
  exactness check and kernel_fn race ~1.2 KB. The branch has 70 B left, and strictly-dead Python is exhausted (the AST
  reachability pass finds nothing else). Room would have to come from **live** code, which this brief did not allow,
  e.g. the prepare-only print diagnostics `_hc_anatomy` (2,296 B) + `_ead_anatomy` (1,812 B), PEP8 blank lines
  (1,580 lines ~1.6 KB), or race arms that never win on the judge. That is the user's call.
- cp: nothing to port (decode's comb cell is the same kernel). Lowering the comb cell's T=256 bar would be worth
  ~0.02%.
- A decode check of campaign/dxl alone would ask a ~+0.1% question. It is better as a rider on the next decode carrier,
  ideally together with ms_align once there is room. Its value on a check is diagnostic: whether T=1 routes an XL pair
  on the judge.
- Not done: no okc run, master untouched.
