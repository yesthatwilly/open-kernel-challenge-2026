## 2. Method (node)

`_d128` runs at the end of prepare (after every routing decision) on master's prepared state, T=128 only:
- 6 judge-shaped draws per replicate (`_ead_cases` + the real router, as `_hcx_inputs`), seeds ..21 / ..22 / ..23.
- H in {-64, -48, -32, -24, -20, -16, -12, -8}. H-64 is **plain all-cold** on 17 of 18 draws (one draw has a 65-route
  expert); H-48 on 13 of 18 x cold arm in {B = v4cxs, E4XL, E5XL (the judge's T=128 route)}.
- Modes, per (H, arm): block (`_hc_experts(part="all")`, tail included) **n** serial normal, **c** serial consume-only
  (wh V78 + ea4x bit 512: no HBM after the first loads, the judge proxy where the H100 HBM wall hides compute), **f**
  forked normal, **g** forked consume-only. The fork is decode's own dead branch (`fork=True` with a `hc["fk"]` dict:
  wh5ds hot half on the W6-M side stream via `_rt_fork_pick`, cold on main, event join, tail without PDL), i.e. exactly
  moe's T=256 shape. Plus cold alone n / c, hot alone n / c, `_hc_dispatch` (align) alone and `_hc_tail` alone.
- One interleaved `_rt_time` per draw over 162 graphs, 8 rounds x 12 reps, read-flushed. Statistic: mean over draws of
  the per-draw median of the per-round paired ratio.
- Exactness: every arm == B bitwise at its H, **fork == serial bitwise** at every H, every H vs H-16 (bitwise or rel-L2
  1-5e-4: hot wgmma vs Engine A order). **All true on all 18 draws**; tickets / err 0, whk_err = [0, WH_MAGIC].
- Draw shapes: r2 / r3 are judge-shaped (156-185 routes, H-16 hot 2-4 experts / 83-124 routes, cold 13-21); r1 drew the
  busier EP ranges (191-235 routes, hot 4-6 / 134-196 routes).

In-layer A/B (`d128-lay-r{1,2}`): one process, master's prepared state, arm `fk` = post-prepare
`hc.fk = {on: True, s: _rt_fork_stream(cuda) (priority 0, distinct from the capture stream), e0, e1}`. That re-enables
decode's dead T=128 fork branch (`HCF_CLS = 128`) with no byte change. verify 16 sliding + full, 20 reps = 10 judge
pairs, fresh judge-like inputs every rep, every replay checked, normal mode (the whole layer cannot be consume-only).

## 5. Key question: why is decode's T=128 not forked like moe's T=256?

1. **It was forked, and the fork was killed on the judge before the current kernels existed.** W6-F (10-27, check
   ee9142eb) raced H-16f inside decode's T=128 layer gate with the wg hot half and the v3/v4 cold half. The block read
   x0.93, but the layer read H-16f x1.003 vs master on the judge (CLAIMS 67, LOG 1072-1074): "the x0.93 block gain does
   not survive inside the layer". moe's W6-M fork passed its kernel_fn race at M=256 (x1.024-1.060) and landed. moe's
   T=128 stayed plain (H-16f x0.980).
2. **The fork setter was later trimmed as dead Python.** Only the read side is left: `_hc_experts` reads
   `state["hc"].get("fk")`, and `HCF_CLS = 128`. Nothing in master sets `fk`, so `fk` is None on both the node and the
   judge (PATH `fk None`). The landed `[w6m-fork]` gate on decode is a different fork: the router / align side stream
   (`side_serial`), not hot vs cold.
3. **Nothing re-raced it after the kernels changed.** wh5ds (hot pair x0.79), v4cxs (10-29) and E5XL (10-30) all
   landed later. moe's t128h lane showed that the cold-half change turned moe's forked T=128 hybrid from a loss into a
   0.934 mean. The 10-28 fork-priority incident (priority -1 serialized moe's fork on the judge) is fixed in master
   (`RT_FORK_PRIO = 0`), and the v4xd-forkguard identity guard is landed.
4. **Mechanism limit.** The halves cannot co-reside (h100-expert-1028: a wh5ds CTA needs a whole SM, and cold w13 fills
   every SM). So the fork overlaps only the ramps, the tails and the cold half's HBM stalls. That is why consume-only,
   the judge proxy, gives just 3.5-7% of the block while the H100's normal mode gives 7-9%.

## 6. Lever sizing (judge-scaled) and verdict

| lever | node evidence | judge-scaled d (us, T=128 block) | headline |
|---|---|---|---|
| **T=128 hot/cold fork** (re-enable `fk` at HCF_CLS, prio-0 stream, layer veto) | block g/c .927 / .965 / .967; f/n .91-.93; in-layer x1.049-1.063 sliding, x1.024-1.048 full | **~6-12** (c-mode and judge HBM headroom); ceiling 14-24 (node normal, in-layer) | **~+0.3-0.6%**; ceiling +0.94% |
| a different split H (with or without the fork) | no H beats H-16 in every replicate; fork optimum H-16 | 0 | 0 |
| plain all-cold (H-64) | +2.4..+7% c, +8..+18% n | negative | - |
| E4XL instead of E5XL at T=128 under the fork | g .954-.963 vs .965-.967 | ~1-2 | ~0.05-0.1% |
| hot half's fixed cost (empty-launch floor) | 6.6 us node with no hot work | <= 6 | <= 0.3% (needs a new kernel) |

Judge scaling: the judge's cold half runs at 76% of the ring, against the node's ~35% stall in normal mode, so the judge
sits near the consume-only reading (block -4..-9 us). The in-layer node figure (-14..-24 us) is an upper bound.
Precedent pulls the estimate down further: on 10-27 the judge's layer read a block-level x0.93 fork as x1.003, and the
node and judge have disagreed on the sign of hybrid races before (moe CLAIMS 65).

**Verdict (pre-registered rule): NULL.** No lever reaches 2% headline. The biggest is the T=128 hot/cold fork, at
~0.3-0.6% judge-scaled. Its ceiling is +0.94% (the node's in-layer normal-mode gain, lay-r2). It is bit-exact
(fork == serial on all 18 draws), and every node round is < 1. So there is no `.worktrees/d128`, no `campaign/d128` and no
plan. Master is untouched.

**Rider (recommended for the next decode stack, T=128 code only):** restore the `fk` setter for `HCF_CLS = 128` at the
end of prepare:
- fork stream from `_rt_fork_stream` (priority 0, identity-guarded);
- a judge-side layer gate, fork vs serial twin <= 0.99 in every round, through `_ead_layer_gate` the way `_sfk_gate`
  does, with fallback serial.
It costs a few hundred bytes, which need dead-Python trims (coordinate with dxl's trims). If it lands at the node's
in-layer reading, the expected value is +0.3-0.9%.

Reopen as a stand-alone lever only with a judge read of the T=128 fork in the layer >= 1.04 on verify 16 sliding. A rider
check gives that read for free.
