## 0. Pre-registration (from the brief)

**Question.** Can more independent work per SMSP cut the N <= 64 hot-half stage toward its floor?
**Decision rule.** Forked pair vs wh (mean over dec a/b/c) at T=256 or T=128, or the moe draw:
<= 0.95 -> decode/moe check candidate stacking on wh; 0.95-0.98 -> rides with wh's next revision; > 0.98 -> the N <= 64 hot
half is at its practical form; say why (ncu).
Exactness: every timed variant bit-identical to master (dq0) on w13 and w2 in all 8 routings.

**(b) third consumer warpgroup: not built.** The tile is 128 weight rows = 2 x wgmma m64; a third WG has no rows without a
192-row bank layout, and a k-split across WGs is not bit-exact (two fp32 partial sums). The ping-pong (pp/ppa) tests the same
mechanism (more consumer work co-resident per SMSP) without a layout change.
**(c) 2 CTAs/SM: not built.** wg_kernel takes 187-204 KB dynamic + 8 KB static smem and 384 x 168 regs: 1 CTA/SM. Two need
<= ~113 KB each (2 stages + an epilogue <= 29 KB; w2's is 35 KB) and <= 85 launch regs (consumers <= 112 via setmaxnreg), which
the N >= 80 ring path (64-reg accumulator + ring) cannot hold; the forked cold half (2 x 112 KB CTAs/SM) also wants that
residency. Section 3 shows co-scheduled dequant + RS-wgmma on one SMSP does not overlap (pp, dov2), which predicts no gain from a
second CTA either.

## 3. Why (ptxas, rungs, ncu)

- **The stage is additive.** combined ~= dequant-only + MMA-only - ring skeleton: N32 632 + 504 - 387 = 749 vs 787; N64 631 +
  586 - 365 = 852 vs 931-975. The two consumer WGs already run their dequant and MMA phases about in lockstep.
- **Running the dequant concurrently with an RS wgmma group on the same SMSP makes it slower, not hidden:**
  - dov2 (an independent dequant placed under a fixed-A 8-wgmma group, the ideal case) is 906 vs the serial 787 at N32;
  - ping-pong (other WG's dequant under this WG's group) 891 / 1085;
  - record pipelining (p2h / p2hx5d) 886 / 824;
  - wh's per-k16 ring at N48/64 also lands at ~dequant + MMA.
  This is consistent with ALU dequant and the register-sourced A reads of an in-flight wgmma competing on the SMSP
  (register-file ports). It is an inference from the timings, not a direct counter.
- **The MMA side is a dependent accumulator chain, not tensor throughput.** MMA-only at N32 is 504 cyc per 8 k16 (63 per
  wgmma) against 16 tensor cycles each; at N64 586 (73 vs 32). Only more groups in flight would hide it, and:
- **ptxas refuses 16 wgmma in flight at 168 regs.** p2, p2x5d, p2k (no keep idiom), p2s (p2 only at N16/32: 8-16 accumulator
  regs) and dmop (fixed A, nothing written) all get **C7512 "wgmma serialized due to insufficient register resources"**, and
  SASS shows `WARPGROUP.DEPBAR.LE gsb0, 0x0` after every HGMMA. It also serializes the function's N >= 80 ring (p2 N80 1237 vs
  974 in the quick run). Eight in flight (p2h, a3 loop, ring) is clean.
- **ptxas pitfall (method).** ddq and dov as first built consumed the dequant with `asm volatile("" :: "r"(x))`. That emits no
  PTX use, so ptxas dropped the dequant (0 HMUL2, a 67-instruction loop). That is how the ring-only skeleton was found. ddq2 / dov2
  fold the values with real LOP3s and a never-taken store.

  1 CTA/SM [hbm].
- **What is left for N <= 64** (none built here):
  - fewer dequant ALU ops (rgx2's exact-cover puts ~9 ops/word at the floor; A5b is there);
  - A from shared memory (SS wgmma: no RF A reads, but >= 32 KB/stage of STS plus the tensor's smem reads; likely smem-bound
    at N32);
  - hh's scheduling lever (22-27% tail / wave quantization), which sits outside the stage.

## 4. What transfers to the H200 judge

- **Transfers [cyc]:**
  - the K-step and rung tables (per-SM cycles at 1830 MHz);
  - the additive-stage structure and the dov2 / pp non-overlap;
  - the isolated pair ratios;
  - the ncu stall structure;
  - ptxas C7512 on 16 in-flight wgmma at 168 regs (a compile-time property of the same sm_90a cu130 build);
  - bit-exactness.
- **Does not transfer:**
  - absolute us;
  - the fork-bw arm;
  - the **ring-only skeleton** (387 cyc is HBM latency; H200 HBM3e differs);
  - the forked ratios (a judge-shaped model, no ALU / L2 contention from the real ea4x cold kernels). None is near 0.95, so
    the verdict does not hinge on them.
