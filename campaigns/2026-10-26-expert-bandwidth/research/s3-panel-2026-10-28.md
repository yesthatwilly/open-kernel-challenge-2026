Where the designs disagreed, these settle it:

1. **"Spread each expert over more CTAs / split-K"** (LOG 2200Z) is the wrong direction. The cross-CTA split is the cost: fix-ups, n=3's idle 72 K2 CTAs, and K2's late placement. All three designs found this; the code confirms it.
2. **Design 2's D=5 ring is dropped.** Depth does not matter (ladder), and a 184 KB ring rules out a co-resident K2.
3. **Design 3's "K2 fits beside K1" holds** with the layout in §6:
   - smem: 127,328 + 90,448 + 2 KB reserved = 219.8 KB <= 228 KB.
   - registers: <= 96 per thread at 320 threads x 2 CTAs.
4. **Design 2's "T=8 -8 us" is optimistic.**
   - At n=8, v4's split is already about balanced: w13 15.5 -> 16 units plus w2 8 units ≈ 24 x 2.82 us ≈ 67.7 us.
   - v6 there is 32 + 16 records x ~1.41 us ≈ 67.7 us [E].
   - At T=8, v6 removes only the fix-ups (~2-4 us) and the per-stage x copies (~3%) [E]. Class 8 is a near tie.
5. **New hazard: a 90-107 KB K2 CTA can double up on an SM.** This happens at T=1..3 when K1 CTAs exit one by one and pending K2 CTAs land on the freed SMs. The per-expert K2 split assumes one CTA per SM, so K2's request is padded to >= 116 KB whenever co-residency is not intended. The 132-CTA v1 K2 loss is consistent with such doubling, but that is not shown [E].
6. **`griddepcontrol.launch_dependents`** may count per CTA on its first invocation, or once all threads have called it. The trigger points in §6 are safe under both readings.
7. **A late extra waiter on `full[0]` could alias parity.** So the epilogue warp triggers after its first `pfull` wait, never on `full[0]`.

## 2. The three designs in short

**D1 "eask" (splitk) + "v4d".**
- eask is a T=1-only single launch with h-aligned split-K: each CTA computes 16 h columns over the full K, then the w2 K-slice for those columns. The pieces:
  - a new lossless w2 repack (+302 MB);
  - fp32 partials [264][2][4096];
  - a reduction kernel;
  - then `ea_combine`.
- No grid wait between w13 and w2, no tickets.
- v4d (T=2..16) defers v4's first fix-up and balances the w2 split, inside `ea4_kernels.cuh`.
- Its own estimate: T=1 -3.8 / -8.0 / -6.4 us at n = 2/3/4, moe +2.7-3.1%; v4d ≈ -2.4 us at T=8.

**D2 "mlp", Engine A v6.**
- One CTA per SM, 320 threads: 8 consumer warps (warp w = chunk w of every 16-row record), 1 producer, 1 epilogue warp that sums the 8 partials in a fixed order.
- B (x / h) stays resident, chunk-major, so `eak::consume` is unchanged.
- No cross-CTA split, no tickets. D=5 ring at T=1.
- v6p adds an L2 run-ahead arm.
- Raced at T=1 and T=2..8 with a P(n)-weighted rule and per-n floors.
- Its own estimate: T=1 -3.2 us weighted, T=8 -8 us, moe +2.9%.

**D3 "sched", Engine A v6.**
- One CTA per SM, 288 threads. Whole-K 16-row tiles are owned by contiguous warp runs, with boundary partials reduced at the end.
- K2 runs in per-expert CTA blocks.
- At T=1, K2 is made co-resident with K1. K1 triggers PDL after its first stage; K2 prefetches its first records, then waits.
- The v6s arm (K2 without PDL) prices the co-residency.
- A v6-only copy check runs before routing.
- Its own estimate: T=1 -6.7 us weighted (~x1.20), moe +3.1%; T=8 -3 to -8 us.

## 3. Scores (0-10)

| design | gain | evidence | risk (10 = safe) | build effort (10 = easy) | decode port | **overall** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 eask + v4d | 6 | 8 | 3 | 3 | 4 | **4.5** |
| D2 v6 mlp | 6 | 8 | 6 | 5 | 7 | **6.0** |
| D3 v6 sched | 7 | 8 | 6 | 5 | 8 | **7.0** |

**D1.**
- Strongest diagnosis: its fit T = 13.0 + 1.30 x stage-times + fix-ups reproduces every judge row.
- But eask's fixed-cost edge over a co-resident-K2 v6 is ≤ ~1-2 us [E]. It costs three new pieces, needs a new repack, and is T=1 only.
- v4d edits the T=2..32 incumbent's own file: a compile error there costs v4 through the fallback chain.

**D2.**
- Its within-CTA mechanics are the simplest to build blind: a uniform per-record pipeline with no boundary cases.
- But D=5 forfeits the co-resident K2, the T=8 claim is optimistic, and v6p is expected to tie.

**D3.**
- Best architecture: whole-record ownership, a co-resident K2 that attacks the measured 3.8 us K1->K2 gap, a diagnostic arm, and a v6-only copy check.
- Its contiguous-run split carries boundary bookkeeping, which D2's per-chunk split avoids.

## 5. Check question and kill rule

**Question.** Does Engine A v6 cut moe's T=1 expert block at n = 2/3/4 from 29.0 / 41.2 / 47.9 us (board, net), and bring the cost per extra local expert from ~9.9 us (steps 12.1 / 6.7 / 12.1) to <= 8.0 us? v6 here is whole 16-row records on one 320-thread CTA per SM with 8 consumer warps, no cross-CTA split-K or tickets, resident B, and at T=1 a K2 co-resident with K1. How much of the gain is the co-resident K2 (v6 vs v6s), and does the class-8 arm beat the incumbent at T=8?

**Decision on the result.**
- (a) **v6 or v6s routes at T=1** (weighted >= 1.03 in every round, floors held, copy PASS): send a scored run if the check's moe headline beats 1.7225 (DAILY-RHYTHM). Then port the winner to decode's d1 rows (whole-layer gate, K1 with pdl13).
- (b) **Read v6's us per extra expert** (OLS n=2..5):
  - <= 8.0: the staircase was the split. The rest of the way to 7.5 is consumer instructions per weight element.
  - 8.0-9.0: read the anatomy (ramp, K1->K2 gap, epilogue lag, record time) before any rework.
  - >= 9.0: kill rule (2).
- (c) **Co-residency:** v6/v6s at n=2 >= x1.04 keeps the co-resident K2 in the decode port. <= x1.01 ports the simpler v6s launch.
- (d) **Class 8** routes only on its own gate. Its p10 / p50 / p90 times give the T=8 slope. If v6 < x1.00 at p50, class 8 leaves the decode port.

**Kill rule.** Kill v6, S3's re-split line, if this check shows any of:
1. at T=1 neither v6 nor v6s qualifies, and v6's weighted ratio is < 1.00 in either round (a clean loss);
2. v6's measured cost per extra expert, OLS n=2..5 in either round, is >= 9.0 us. The staircase is then not a split artifact: S3 moves to the consumer and v6 is not ported;
3. v6 is slower than the incumbent at n=3 in both rounds.

These outcomes are not a performance verdict: a compile error, a spill at both MINB, a canary FAIL, or a device fault. They get one fix and one re-check with new bytes; a second failure of the same kind kills.

A near-miss (weighted 1.00-1.03 with slope < 9.0) is never re-rolled with the same bytes. It gets one rework only if the anatomy names a cost >= 2 us at n=2 or n=3 with a concrete fix.

### 6.2 Kernel: `ea6_kernels.cuh` (namespace `eak6`, sm_90a, dq2 only)

**Reuse, unchanged:**
- namespace `ea` (`ea_common.cuh`);
- `eak::consume<2>`, `eak::build_map`, `eak::silu_mul`;
- `eak::REC` 4608, `STAGE_W` 18432, `XROW` 1040, `NK` 8, `KDIM` 4096;
- the v1 repack `ea["w13r"]` / `ea["w2r"]`, laid out `[expert][64-row group][512-k chunk][4 warp records][4608 B]`;
- no new GPU memory.

**Geometry.**
- Grid G = SM count (132), one CTA per SM per kernel.
- 320 threads: warps 0-7 consume (warp w always takes 512-k chunk w), warp 8 produces, warp 9 is the epilogue.
- A record is one 16-row warp record over the full K: 8 pieces of 4608 B. Piece kc of record (le, gi, wq) is at `wr + ((le*NG + gi)*8 + kc)*18432 + wq*4608`, with NG = 128 (w13) or 64 (w2).
- Ring depth in records: K1 D=3, K2 D=2.

**Template.**
- `template <bool W13, int D, int MINB> __global__ void __launch_bounds__(320, MINB) ea6_kernel(const __nv_bfloat16* bsrc, const int* topk_ids, const uint8_t* wr, const float* gs, __nv_bfloat16* out, int* local_ids, int* err, long long* dbg, int T, int start, int count, int flags)`.
- Instances: (true,3,2), (true,3,1), (false,2,2), (false,2,1). MINB=1 is only a spill fallback.

**Shared memory** (dynamic, T-dependent, base 128-aligned), in this order:
- ring D x 36,864;
- B, 8 x T x 1,040, chunk-major: token t of chunk kc at (kc·T + t)·1040, so consume reads chunk kc with `xs = B + kc·T·1040`;
- P: 2 x 8 warps x 32 lanes float4 = 8,192;
- mbarriers full[D], empty[D], bfull, pfull[2], pfree[2];
- ints s_n, s_act[32] (136 B).

`smem6 = D·36864 + 8320·T + 8192 + 8·(2D+5) + 136`:

| kernel | T=1 | T=8 |
|---|---|---|
| K1 | 127,328 | 185,568 |
| K2 | 90,448 | 148,688 |

Launch request:
- **K1:** smem6. At >= 127 KB, it is one CTA per SM.
- **K2 at T=1 with PDL:** exactly smem6, so it fits beside K1 (219,824 <= 233,472).
- **Every other K2 launch** (T >= 2, or without PDL): max(smem6, 118,784), so two K2 CTAs never share an SM.

**Prologue.**
1. `if (W13 && (flags & 4)) griddep_wait();` (decode only; moe passes 0).
2. warp 0: `eak::build_map(topk_ids, T, start, count, lane, s_act, &s_n, local_ids, W13 && blockIdx.x == 0)`.
3. thread 32: `mbar_init` full[d] = 1, empty[d] = 8, bfull = 1, pfull[k] = 8, pfree[k] = 1; then `fence_barrier_init()`.
4. `__syncthreads()`.

**Split** (n = s_n, c = blockIdx.x):
- **K1:** R = 512n records, flat q = a·512 + rt; CTA c takes [⌊cR/G⌋, ⌊(c+1)R/G⌋).
- **K2:** per-expert CTA blocks, one h per CTA:
  - a = ⌊c·n/G⌋, c0 = ⌈G·a/n⌉, c1 = ⌈G·(a+1)/n⌉, m = c1 − c0, j = c − c0;
  - the CTA takes records [⌊256j/m⌋, ⌊256(j+1)/m⌋) of expert a.
- In both: gi = rt>>2, wq = rt&3, le = s_act[a].
- At T=1, max records per CTA (K1 + K2) = 12 / 18 / 24 / 30 at n = 2..5.
- **n = 0:** K2 calls `griddep_wait()`; every thread calls `griddep_launch()` and returns.

**Producer (warp 8).**
- **issue(i):**
  1. slot = i % D;
  2. lane 0 `mbar_expect_tx(full[slot], 36864)`; `__syncwarp()`;
  3. lanes 0..7 `bulk_g2s(ring + slot·36864 + lane·4608, piece(record b+i, kc = lane), 4608, full[slot])`.
- **K1:**
  1. lane 0 `expect_tx(bfull, 8T·1024)`; `__syncwarp()`;
  2. issue the 8T B copies of 1024 B (x + t·4096 + kc·512 → B + (kc·T+t)·1040);
  3. issue records 0..min(r,D)−1;
  4. `mbar_wait(full[0], 0)`; `griddep_launch()`;
  5. for i >= D: `mbar_wait(empty[slot], ((i/D)−1)&1)`, then issue(i).
- **K2:**
  1. issue records 0..min(r,D)−1 first (the weights do not depend on K1);
  2. `griddep_wait()`; `griddep_launch()`;
  3. copy h from h + (a·T+t)·4096 + kc·512 into B on bfull;
  4. issue the rest as in K1.

**Consumers (warps 0-7).**
- K2 only: `griddep_wait(); griddep_launch();` first, so they park while a co-resident K1 runs.
- `mbar_wait(bfull, 0)`.
- For i in [0, r):
  1. slot = i%D; `mbar_wait(full[slot], (i/D)&1)`;
  2. `if (W13 && i == 0) griddep_launch();`
  3. `float acc[2][4] = {}`; `eak::consume<2>(ring + slot·36864 + w·4608, smem_u32(B) + w·T·1040, lane, T, acc)`;
  4. `__syncwarp()`; lane 0 `mbar_arrive(empty[slot])`;
  5. `c[k] = acc[0][k] + acc[1][k]`;
  6. `if (i >= 2) mbar_wait(pfree[i&1], ((i>>1)−1)&1)`;
  7. `P[i&1][w][lane] = float4(c)`; `__syncwarp()`; lane 0 `mbar_arrive(pfull[i&1])`.

**Epilogue (warp 9).**
- K2 only: `griddep_wait(); griddep_launch();` first.
- For i in [0, r):
  1. `mbar_wait(pfull[i&1], (i>>1)&1)`;
  2. `if (W13 && i == 0) griddep_launch();`
  3. s = P[i&1][0][lane]; then s += P[i&1][w][lane] for w = 1..7 in order (fp32, component-wise);
  4. `__syncwarp()`; lane 0 `mbar_arrive(pfree[i&1])`;
  5. v1's epilogue (`ea_kernels.cuh`:293-320) with warp → wq and c → s, g = lane>>2, cq = lane&3:
     - **K1:** col = gi·32 + wq·8 + g. For j < 2 and t = 2cq+j < T: `h[(a·T+t)·4096 + col] = bf16(silu_mul(s[j]·sc, s[2+j]·sc))`, sc = gs[le].
     - **K2:** n0 = gi·64 + wq·16 + g; v0 = bf16(s[j]·sc), v1 = bf16(s[2+j]·sc). For every slot s' < 8 with `topk_ids[t·8+s'] − start == le`: write `y[(t·8+s')·4096 + n0] = v0` and `[.. + n0 + 8] = v1`.

**fp32 order** (fixed, which the copy check needs):
- per warp, consume's two k16-parity chains over its chunk, then acc0 + acc1;
- across chunks, ((p0+p1)+p2)…+p7.
- No atomics, tickets, workspace, or state that persists between launches.

**Proof obligations** (to show in the emulator):
- Consumers need full(i) and pfree(i−2). The epilogue needs pfull(i). The producer needs empty(i−D). There is no cycle.
- No waiter falls two phases behind, so parities never alias.
- All waits are `ea::mbar_wait` (bounded, error word 1).
- No CTA ever waits on another CTA, so co-residency is never needed for correctness.

**PDL.**
- K1 triggers once its first record lands, at the trigger points above. These are safe whether the hardware counts the first invocation per CTA or waits for all threads.
- At T=1, K2 (PDL attribute when flags & 1) then lands one CTA beside each K1 CTA. It builds its map, prefetches 2 records and parks on `griddepcontrol.wait`.
- **v6s** = the same kernels, with K2 launched without the PDL attribute.

**Debug** (prepare only; nullptr in the timed path). Per CTA, 8 int64 at `dbg[((W13?0:1)·G + c)·8 + k]`, all `%globaltimer` (the form eaf ran on the judge):
- [0] entry, thread 0;
- [1] first full[0] seen, warp 0 lane 0;
- [2] last consume done, warp 0 lane 0;
- [3] last epilogue done, warp 9 lane 0;
- [4] r;
- [5] K2's `griddep_wait` returned, warp 8 lane 0.

**Host.**
- `ea6_info()`:
  - for each instance, set `MaxDynamicSharedMemorySize` to 185,568 (K1) or 148,688 (K2);
  - set `PreferredSharedMemoryCarveout = cudaSharedmemCarveoutMaxShared`;
  - return per instance: ok, numRegs, localSizeBytes, smem at T=1, smem at T=8, occupancy at the T=1 request.
- Entry points:
  - `ea6_w13(x, topk_ids, wr, gs, h, local_ids, err, dbg, T, start, count, grid, minb, flags)`;
  - `ea6_w2(h, topk_ids, wr, gs, y, err, dbg, T, start, count, grid, minb, flags)`.
- Checks as in `ea_check_common`: T 1..8; count 1..32; `wr.numel() == count·NG·8·18432`; h >= 32·T·4096; y >= 8T·4096; dbg empty or >= 2·grid·8.
- Launch: `cudaLaunchKernelEx`, 320 threads, the smem request above, the current stream. K1 gets programmatic serialization only if flags & 4; K2 only if flags & 1. Clear and `TORCH_CHECK` launch errors as v1 does.

**Build.**
- `_ea6_build_start(t_prepare)`:
  - sets `TORCH_CUDA_ARCH_LIST=9.0a`;
  - starts a daemon thread running `_rt_build(EA6_TAG, name, _EA6_CPP, src, ("ea6_info", "ea6_w13", "ea6_w2"))`;

**Forward core.**
- `_ea6_forward_core(ea, x, topk_ids, topk_weights, start, count, norm_w, trace=None, pdl13=0, k2_pdl=1, dbg=None, scratch=None)` keeps v1's contract:
  - allocate h = empty(32, T, I), local_ids = empty(T, 8), y = empty(8T, H); `scratch` passes NaN-poisoned h / y, canary only;
  - `ea6_w13` (flags 4 if pdl13), then `ea6_w2` (flags 1 if k2_pdl);
  - then `ext.ea_combine(..., 1)` if `ea.get("cmb_ok")`, else the Triton `_post_norm_combine_kernel`.
- `_ea6s_forward_core` is the same with k2_pdl = 0.
- trace hooks as in v1.

**`_ea6_stage(weights, bank, state, device, t_prepare)`.** It never raises. It saves prev = `dict(ea["on"])` and restores it on any exception. Each step checks its deadline (start < 225 s, canary and race < 240, copy < 262). A miss skips the step with a line and keeps the incumbents.
1. **Admit and inspect.**
   - Proceed only if dq == 2 and the build joined.
   - From `ea6_info`: MINB = 2 if both MINB-2 instances have 0 spill, else 1.
   - coresident = MINB == 2 and 10·alloc(r13) + 10·alloc(r2) <= 65,536, with alloc(r) = ⌈32r/256⌉·256.
   - Store `ea["ext6"]`, `ea6_minb`, `ea6_grid` = SMs, and `ea6_nodbg` = `torch.empty(0, int64)`.
2. **Canary** (eager, synced; the first launch of each arm traced with `_rt_tracer`):
   - Coverage:
     - v6 at T=1..8; v6s at T=1;
     - T=1: every `_ea_routings` kind plus forced n = 3, 4, 5;
     - T=8: every kind;
     - T=2..7: zipf, zero-local, max-active.
   - h and y start as NaN.
   - Pass requires:
     - error word 0;
     - `_rt_check` within half the judge's bounds;
     - rel <= 2·max(master, 2e-3);
     - exact zeros without local routes;
     - v6 at T=1 and T=8 run twice gives bitwise-equal output.
   - A FAIL at any T removes that arm from its class.
3. **Kernel-only** (read flush, 21 reps): at T=1, forced n = 2 and 3. Time v1's K1 / K2 alone (as `_ea_kernel_rates` does) when the incumbent is v1, and v6's K1 / K2 alone (K2 without PDL).
4. **Race** (kernel_fn graphs; `_rt_time` with read flush, 41 reps x 2 rounds, baseline subtracted):
   - **Class 1:**
     - incumbent = `ea["on"][1]` (False = master);
     - arms v6 and v6s;
     - inputs: forced n = 1..5 at T=1 (n distinct local ids plus 8 − n distinct remote ids, shuffled), seed 20261060.
   - **Class 8:**
     - incumbent = `ea["on"][8]`; arm v6;
     - inputs: 15 `_eb_ref_ids(8, gen, "normal", start, count)` draws sorted by distinct local experts; take indices 1 / 7 / 13, equal weights.
   - **Qualify** in every round:
     - every input meets its floor;
     - the weighted ratio is >= 1.03, or >= 1.02 when every input's arm IQR is <= 1.5 x max(incumbent IQR, 0.5 us).
   - **Winner:** the qualifier with the best worst-round weighted ratio.
5. **Anatomy** (optional, `EA6_ANATOMY`; eager, synced, inputs held, try/except; never routes): at T=1, n = 2 and 3, for v6 and v6s:
   - K1 ramp, us per record, end spread;
   - K2 entry, wait-return and first record relative to K1's last exit;
   - K2 us per record and tail;
   - the share of K2 CTAs that entered before K1 ended.
6. **v6-only copy check** for each winning class (T = 1 or 8):
   - two states: `state` and `_wg_copy_tree(state)`;
   - 4 refills, alternating the replay order, each against eager;
   - pass at rel <= `EA_COPY_TOL`; a miss keeps that class on its incumbent.
7. **Route:** `ea["on"][1]` = the class-1 winner; `ea["on"][2..8]` = the class-8 winner.
