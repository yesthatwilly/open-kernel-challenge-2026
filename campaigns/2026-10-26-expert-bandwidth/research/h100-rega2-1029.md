## Question (pre-registered)

What beats A3 (dq2: ext_prop codes plus the paired-scale prmt/IMAD) on the rega tile?

**Decision rule:**

- A w13+w2 pair that beats A3 by >= 5% at low or high, bit-exact with no spill, becomes a 10-30 judge build candidate,
  stacked on A3.
- A schedule that now wins with A3 reopens the CLAIMS overlap levers.
- Nothing: A3 is the tile's practical form. Then say where ncu says the time goes.

## Arms

All arms are bit-exact against master.

| arm | what changes vs A3 |
|---|---|
| **A4** | New lossless **dq4 code encoding**: a prepare-time bit permutation of each code word, `ENC4` in rgx2.cu, found by exact-cover search. Pairs: pair 0 `q & M`; pair 1 `(q<<6) & M`; pair 2 `rotl(q,10) & M`; pair 3 `((q<<4) & 0x81808180) \| ((q>>4) & 0x00400040)`. That is 9 ops/word vs ext_prop's 11. Search result: no encoding reaches 8 ops with shift/rotate/PRMT views, including two-word funnels. A4 forces the `>>4` through `__umulhi` (IMAD.HI) to put it on the FMA pipe. |
| **A4b** | A4 with the `>>4` as a plain SHF. A3's scale path is unchanged, so only the code permutation table differs from A3. |
| **A5b** | A4b codes plus a **one-LOP3 scale**. Each scale byte b in [0x08, 0x7E] is stored as the 7-bit field b-8, which is its exponent field minus 1. The fields are bit-packed: k even at bits 4-10 / 20-26, k odd at a byte rotate of those. `sp = (w & 0x07F007F0) \| 0x78007800` (inline lop3 0xEA, constant held in a register) equals A3's `t*16 + 0x77807780 = e4m3(b)*2^119` exactly. One LOP3 (+ PRMT on odd k) replaces PRMT + IMAD. The lossless repack has A3's range precondition. |
| **D** | The wgmma descriptor's low word is computed once per stage, plus a constant per wgmma, instead of master's `make_desc` per call. This cuts the uniform-datapath instructions. |
| **SC** | **Split commit.** Record 0 dequant, 4 wgmma, commit; then record 1 dequant into a0[4..7] while that group runs; then 4 wgmma, commit, wait 0. No extra registers, no C75xx warnings. SC64 = split only for N >= 64. |
| **RR** | **Register ring.** This is the deferred `wgmma_wait`: stage s+1's dequant goes into a second A set under stage s's group. It applies to N <= 64 only, because N128 hit ptxas C7512 at the kernel's 168-register compile budget. The full-barrier wait and all branches sit outside the async window, and SASS is serialization-free. |

All arms ran at **168 regs, 0 local, occupancy 1**, and were bit-exact on every EXACT line in every run. That is every arm
vs master, w13 and w2, at low (T 753), high (3012) and ft (16384, `rega_kernel<G,true>`), on both banks, with pop and random
routing.

### Readings

- `[cyc]` **IMAD.HI is expensive.** A4 differs from A4b only in using `__umulhi` (IMAD.HI.U32) for one shift. It lost
  x1.03-1.11 even with fewer instructions. Keep right shifts on SHF. This matters for moe too.
- `[cyc]` **D** is worth ~3% at low (uniform-pipe issue: 23.4M -> 11.9M uniform instr per launch) and nothing at high.
- `[cyc]` **SC is the overlap lever that works, and only with fewer issued instructions.** On its own (A3+SC) it gains
  3.6-4.1% at high and ft but loses 2-6% at low (low w2 x1.06). Stacked on D it gains everywhere (A3+D+SC low 0.96). With
  A5b+D it gains 7.7% at low L2.
- `[cyc]` **RR loses** (x1.04-1.12 at low, neutral at high/ft), even serialization-free (no C7512, clean SASS). That
  answers pf-rega-low-stream's reopen condition ("a ring that ptxas does not serialize"): it was not serialization. RR
  stays refuted.

## Verdict (per the rule)

- **A5b+D+SC beats A3 by >= 5% at high** (both banks, 0.933-0.948) and at low on the L2 bank (0.923-0.925, per-SM view).
  Low HBM is 0.954-0.963 and ft 0.936-0.945. All bit-exact, 168 regs, no spill.
  -> **10-30 judge build candidate, stacked on A3** (A3's routing and range check, with the bank copy re-encoded as dq4 +
  packed scales).
- Expected judge pair vs A3: ~0.93-0.96 at low, ~0.94-0.95 at high. Vs master: ~0.79-0.85 low, ~0.85 high.
- **Cheaper fallback:** A4b+D+SC64 (0.920-0.965 / 0.939-0.952) keeps A3's scale path, so only the code permutation table
  changes from A3's bank copy.
- **Overlap levers:** split commit, stacked on the cheaper issue, now wins, which reopens CLAIMS:73's overlap levers *in
  that form only*. The register ring and (by the earlier judge data) the cross-WG schedules stay closed.
- **Where the time goes next:**
  - Low: per-warp dependency latency ("wait", ~0.93 per issue with only 2 consumer warps per SMSP) plus the per-stage
    wgmma wait. Pure dequant is ALU-pipe-bound.
  - High: the tensor pipe is ~79% active, so the tile is within ~20% of its tensor floor.
  - The next prefill lever is not more dequant cuts. Candidates: more TLP on the consumer SMSPs, or something outside the
    rega tile.

## Caveats

- Synthetic bank (uniform codes, scale bytes in [0x08, 0x7E]); exactness is proven on these data, three classes, two seeds.
- A5b's scale repack and A4b/A5b's code encoding are prepare-time lossless re-encodings. A5b drops the scale sign bit:
  the [0x08, 0x7E] range check that A3 already needs guarantees it is 0.
- Clocks were not locked (1.75-1.83 GHz). ft medians are noisy (+-10% round spread).
- Host-side align, as in rgx. No CUDA graphs.
- RGX_* environment overrides do not reach the node: the confirmation run used the script defaults (seed 5, 9 rounds).
