## 1. What was built

**C, the XL trim of `consume2`.**
- `consume2_lt<DQ>` is `consume_e<DQ, 0, 2>`, the two n8 tiles, run on the XL loop's carried `recl` / `recs` / `xb` /
  `m16` / `m78` instead of `eak3::consume2`'s per-record prologue. Its text is generated from `_EAE_LT`'s `consume_lt`.
- `setpass` gives `xb` consume2's B row, `(lane & 7) + 8 (lane >> 4)` clamped to `nr - 1`, in two-tile passes.
- The loads, order and arithmetic are unchanged.

**N, the run-end exchange without `bar_consumers`.**
- Each warp owns a 4-bit field of the super-group's ticket. The static_assert `NK = 8 <= 15` covers it: a super-group
  has at most 8 contributors.
- The warp then runs: its partial stores, then `__syncwarp`, then lane 0's `fence.acq_rel.gpu` + `atomicAdd(1 << 4w)`.
  The last arriver fences again, broadcasts with `__shfl_sync` + `__syncwarp`, and finishes only its own rows. `finish`
  reads only warp `w`'s partials, and every CTA's warp `w` owns the same rows.
- Finally the warp clears its field with `atomicAnd`.
- It adds no spin-waits, so it adds no new hang surface.

**Bench arms (DQ bits on eaenc2's XL):**
- E4XL 27 / E5XL 28 (the base);
- C: E4XC 59 / E5XC 60;
- N: E4XN 91 / E5XN 92;
- C+N: E4XCN 123 / E5XCN 124.

The branch's `'4xr'` module is `_eae_src4xl` + `consume2_lt` + `_xlr_loop` with `XR_C2 = true`, `XR_NB = false`
(§3). It is built in its own prepare thread, alongside 4x / 4xl / 6. EAE_ARMS 7 / 8 = E4XR / E5XR (module
`extexr`, XL bit).

**Readings:**
- **C works only through codegen.** E5XC gains 1.1-1.3% at every T, including T16, where two-tile records are rare (0%
  at T ≤ 16, 6% at T32 per xlncu). So most of the gain comes from ptxas's different allocation of the XL loop once
  `consume2`'s prologue leaves it (K2 E5 92 -> 91 regs). Only ~0.2-0.3 points more appear where two-tile records
  dominate (T128 / T256).
- **E4 does not gain from C.** E4XC is -0.6% at T16 and +0.3-0.4% at T128 / T256. xlncu's "~18 excess instructions per
  two-tile record ≈ 0.5-1% at T256" lands at 0.4% for E4 and is mixed into E5's codegen gain.
- **N is null.** It reads 1.000 ± 0.005 everywhere, and E4XN +0.5% at T16 is the best cell. Removing the run-end
  barrier just moves the slow-warp wait to the next full-barrier `try_wait`. That is xlncu §3's coupling: one full /
  empty pair per 36,864 B stage and a 2-slot ring that cannot deepen. C+N ≈ C.
